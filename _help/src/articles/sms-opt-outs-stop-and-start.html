A contact who replies STOP stops getting your SMS, from every number in your workspace, until they reply START.

When a contact replies STOP

  • A reply of STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT or a similar word opts the contact out.
  • From then on, an SMS to them is refused before it goes, from any of your numbers, and no automatic reply is sent to them. Their conversation shows Opted out.
  • On a number outside the US and Canada, a reply counts only when the whole SMS is one such word: "Stop." counts, "please stop texting me" doesn't. No confirmation SMS goes back to the contact.

Opt back in: when a contact replies START

A reply of START, YES or UNSTOP lifts the opt-out, and you can send them SMS again.

When a contact replies HELP

A contact who replies HELP isn't opted out. Their SMS lands in the Inbox like any other, so answer it there.

Unassign or archive on opt-out

Both are in Settings → Messaging settings → Messaging:

  • Unassign when the contact opts out, under Conversations (on by default): the contact's conversations are unassigned on every number.
  • Archive the conversation when the contact opts out, set for each number under Out-of-hours reply: the contact's conversation on that number moves to Archived.

The list of opted-out contacts

  1. Open Settings → Messaging settings → Messaging.
  2. Under Opt-outs, the count of opted-out contacts shows. Click View list.

Opted-out contacts lists each contact's number and when they opted out. A contact the carriers blocked shows Blocked by the carrier.
