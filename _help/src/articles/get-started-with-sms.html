CallForges sends and receives SMS from your phone numbers. A contact's replies come back to the same conversation, and every conversation is in the Inbox, under Messaging in the sidebar. Whether a number can send SMS depends on its country and its type.

Which numbers can send SMS

  • On Phone Numbers, the Capabilities column shows an SMS chip on every number that can send SMS. A grey or red chip opens the reason and the one thing to do next, such as Add to campaign, Register now or Register business.
  • US numbers send SMS once they are registered with the US carriers (A2P 10DLC). See Register for SMS in the US.
  • Numbers in many other countries, such as the UK, Australia, Germany and France, send and receive SMS with no registration. The full list is in Send SMS to contacts in other countries.
  • Mobile numbers in a country that allows SMS can send it, and so can local numbers in the US and Canada. Toll-free numbers and most landline numbers can't.
  • A number you ported in follows the same rules as one you bought.

SMS in the free trial

  • In the free trial you can send SMS from a number in a country that needs no carrier registration, such as the UK, Australia or Germany. Each SMS is paid from your credits, and you can top up credits at any time.
  • Numbers in the US, Puerto Rico and the US Virgin Islands need 10DLC registration first, and you can register during the trial. The carriers' fees are paid by card when you submit. Once its campaign is approved, the number sends SMS in the trial too. See Register for SMS in the US.
  • One thing needs a paid plan: the Send SMS step in a call workflow shows Not included in the trial. Upgrade to use it. until you choose a plan.
  • If the Inbox reads Messaging is switched off for this workspace. Contact support to turn it on., contact support.

Where to see your SMS: the Inbox

  1. Click Inbox, under Messaging in the sidebar. The number beside Inbox counts your unread SMS.
  2. Pick one of your numbers at the top to see its conversations and whether it can send SMS.
  3. Open a conversation to read it and reply. Each conversation is one contact and one of your numbers, so a contact who texts two of your numbers has two conversations.

Messaging's settings are in Settings → Messaging settings: Messaging (who gets a conversation, opt-outs, the out-of-hours reply and signatures) and Saved replies.

What SMS costs

  • Each SMS you send or receive is charged from your credits, at the rate for the country it reaches.
  • A long SMS goes out in parts, and each part is charged as one SMS. See SMS length and sending rules.
  • With 0 credits, an SMS is refused until you add credits.
  • An SMS the carriers fail to deliver can carry a small fee for the failed message, charged from your credits about two days later.
  • Registering US numbers has the carriers' own fees, paid by card, not from your credits. See Registration fees.
