Each number can send an automatic SMS reply to contacts who write to it while it is closed, such as "Thanks for your message. We're closed right now and will reply after 9 AM."

Set up the reply

  1. Open Settings → Messaging settings → Messaging.
  2. Under Out-of-hours reply, pick the number.
  3. Turn on Out-of-hours reply.
  4. Set the number's Opening hours: the open days, their times and the Time zone.
  5. Write the reply in Out-of-hours reply text, up to 320 characters.
  6. Click Save changes.

How often it is sent

  • A contact who writes while the number is closed gets the reply at most once every 24 hours from that number, however many SMS they send.
  • Contacts who replied STOP never get it, and an SMS that says only STOP, START or HELP gets no reply.
  • If the reply can't go out when their SMS arrives, it is tried again until it goes. 24 hours after their SMS it is marked Not sent instead.

Its signature and length

The number's own signature is added to the reply, and the two together must fit in one message of up to 10 parts. See SMS signatures and saved replies.
