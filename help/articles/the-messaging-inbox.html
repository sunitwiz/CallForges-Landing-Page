The Messaging Inbox

The Inbox holds the SMS conversations of the numbers you can use. Each conversation is one contact and one of your numbers, with every SMS between them, sent and received.

Written by CallForges Support · Updated 14 November 2026

Tabs and labels

Pick a number at the top, then a tab:

  • Open: every conversation that isn't closed or archived.
  • Assigned to me and Unassigned.
  • Drafts: the conversations where you started an SMS and didn't send it.
  • Scheduled: the conversations with an SMS waiting to go out.
  • Closed and Archived, under More when the window is narrow.

In the list, a blue dot and a blue time mean unread SMS. Opted out means the contact replied STOP. Delivery failed, in red, means your last SMS didn't arrive: open the conversation to see why and click Try again.

Search and filters

  • Search by name or number.
  • Number, Assigned to and Unread.
  • Replies: With replies shows the conversations where the contact has sent you an SMS at least once; Without replies, those where they never have.
  • The date of the last SMS.

Every filter works with every tab. Click Clear filters to start again.

Assign a conversation

In a conversation, click Assign and pick a user, or pick Unassigned. Who gets a new conversation by itself is a setting: see Who gets a new SMS conversation.

Close, reopen or archive an SMS conversation

  • Close a conversation when it's done. It moves to Closed, and you can reopen it.
  • Archive one to put it away, such as spam or a contact you won't write to again. It leaves Open and Closed for Archived. Unarchive brings it back. An archived conversation can't be closed or reopened until you unarchive it.
  • Both come back by themselves: when the contact sends an SMS again, a closed or archived conversation reopens in Open.
  • Mark as unread to come back to it later.

Bulk actions on many conversations

  1. Tick the conversations you want, up to 100 at a time.
  2. Choose Close, Reopen, Archive, Unarchive, Mark read or Mark unread.

Each conversation changes as if you had done it on its own, so one that can't change doesn't stop the others, and you are told which didn't change and why. Bulk actions don't send SMS: each SMS goes to one contact.

Who sees which numbers

You see the SMS of the numbers you may call from, and you send from them. A user whose role has Read the texts of every number in the workspace sees every number's SMS, a colleague's numbers included. Sending, assigning and closing need Send texts and assign, close, reopen and mark conversations. Roles are set in Settings → Roles & permissions.

Calls aren't in the Inbox

The Inbox holds SMS only. A missed call shows in the bell of each user it rang, and every call is in Call History.
