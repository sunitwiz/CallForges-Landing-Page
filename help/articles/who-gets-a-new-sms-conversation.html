Who gets a new SMS conversation

Who an SMS conversation is assigned to is set in Settings → Messaging settings → Messaging, under Conversations. The setting is the same for every number in the workspace.

Written by CallForges Support · Updated 14 November 2026

In this article
  1. New conversations
  2. When you start a conversation
  3. When a conversation closes or the contact opts out
  4. Who can change these settings

New conversations

When a contact sends you the first SMS, the conversation is assigned by the rule under New conversations. Pick one:

  • Remain assigned (the default): the conversation waits unassigned until a user replies, then stays with whoever replied last. When another user replies, it moves to them.
  • First response: the conversation waits unassigned until a user replies, and the first user who replies keeps it.
  • Round robin: the conversation goes at once to the number's users, in turn. Replies don't move it.

Click Save changes.

When you start a conversation

With Assign a conversation to whoever starts it on (the default), a conversation you start from New message is yours. Turn it off to leave such conversations unassigned.

When a conversation closes or the contact opts out

  • Unassign when a conversation is closed or archived (on by default): closing or archiving a conversation clears its assignee. When the contact sends an SMS again, it reopens unassigned.
  • Unassign when the contact opts out (on by default): when a contact replies STOP, their conversations on every number are unassigned.

Who can change these settings

Anyone who can read SMS sees these settings. Changing them needs Manage texting settings and saved replies in the user's role. To assign one conversation by hand, see Assign a conversation.
