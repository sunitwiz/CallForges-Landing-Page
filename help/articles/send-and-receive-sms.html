Send and receive SMS

Send an SMS to a contact from one of your numbers, and their replies land in the same conversation in the Inbox. You can also schedule an SMS to go out later.

Written by CallForges Support · Updated 14 November 2026

In this article
  1. Send an SMS
  2. Receive SMS and replies from contacts
  3. Delivery status
  4. Long SMS
  5. Schedule an SMS
  6. Drafts

Send an SMS

  1. Click Inbox, under Messaging in the sidebar.
  2. Open the contact's conversation, or click New message.
  3. In New message, pick a contact or type their number, and check From: the number the SMS goes from. It suggests the number that contact last talked to. When more than one of your numbers can send SMS, you can pick another.
  4. Write your SMS. To insert a text you saved, click Saved replies under the box. Click Expand for a bigger box.
  5. Click Send, or press Ctrl + Enter (⌘ + Enter on a Mac). Enter starts a new line.
  • You send from the numbers you may call from.
  • With 0 credits, an SMS is refused until you add credits.
  • A contact who replied STOP can't be sent an SMS until they reply START. See SMS opt-outs.

Receive SMS and replies from contacts

When a contact replies, or sends an SMS to one of your numbers that can receive it, it lands in the Inbox, in that contact's conversation for that number, and shows as unread. A closed or archived conversation reopens when the contact sends an SMS again. Received SMS are charged like the ones you send.

Delivery status

Under each SMS you send, the conversation shows where it is:

  • Sending: on its way.
  • Sent: the carrier took it, and its delivery isn't reported yet.
  • Delivered: it reached the contact's phone.
  • Not delivered: it didn't arrive, and the reason shows under it, such as a phone that is off, a landline or a number that no longer exists. Click Try again to send it once more from the same number. Each SMS can be sent again once.
  • Not confirmed yet: the carrier didn't answer when the SMS was sent, so we are checking whether it went out. It is never sent twice. Within about 10 minutes it shows what happened, or turns to Not delivered with We couldn't confirm this text was sent. Check with the contact before you try again.
  • Scheduled: it goes out at the time shown under it.

Long SMS

The counter under the message box shows how many SMS your message goes out as and the characters used, for example 2 texts · 161 / 306. A long message goes out in parts that the contact's phone joins into one, each part charged as one SMS, up to 10 parts. Your signature counts toward it. See SMS length and sending rules.

Schedule an SMS

  1. Write your SMS, click the arrow beside Send and choose Schedule….
  2. Pick the Date and Time: at least 2 minutes from now and at most a year ahead.
  3. Click Schedule.
  • The SMS shows in the conversation marked Scheduled, and the Scheduled tab lists the conversations that have one.
  • Until it goes out, the user who scheduled it, or a user who can manage texting settings, can edit its text and time or cancel it.
  • At its time it goes from the same number, with the signature of the user who scheduled it. It is checked again then: if the contact replied STOP, the number can no longer send SMS, or your credits ran out, it isn't sent and shows Not delivered with the reason. Credits are charged only for an SMS that goes out.

Drafts

What you write in a conversation and don't send is kept as your draft, on any device, and the conversation shows Draft: in the list. The Drafts tab lists the conversations where you have one. Your drafts are yours alone. Sending the SMS, scheduling it or clearing the box deletes the draft.
