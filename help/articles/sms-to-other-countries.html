Send SMS to contacts in other countries

You can often send SMS to contacts in other countries, but your number doesn't always show. The carriers in the contact's country decide what they see and whether they can reply, by that country's rules.

Written by CallForges Support · Updated 14 November 2026

In this article
  1. Which countries' numbers can send SMS
  2. What a contact in India, France or the UK sees
  3. Contacts in the US
  4. Send from a number in the contact's country
  5. What it costs

Which countries' numbers can send SMS

A number sends SMS when the carriers let it (mobile numbers, and local numbers in the US and Canada) and its country is one of these:

  • Sending and receiving, with no registration: Australia, Austria, Belgium, Brazil, Canada, Chile, the Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Hong Kong, Hungary, Israel, Italy, Jersey, Lithuania, Mexico, Myanmar, the Netherlands, Norway, the Philippines, Poland, Portugal, South Africa, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Thailand and the UK. Canadian numbers send SMS once you have a plan: the free trial doesn't include them.
  • The US, Puerto Rico and the US Virgin Islands: once the number is registered (A2P 10DLC). See Register for SMS in the US.
  • India, Malaysia and Singapore: not yet. Their carriers need a registration CallForges doesn't offer yet.
  • Every other country, such as Ireland, Japan or Iceland: their numbers can't send SMS.

Toll-free numbers and most landline numbers can't send SMS. On Phone Numbers, each number that can has the SMS chip, and the Inbox says why a number can't.

What a contact in India, France or the UK sees

  • Your number, and they can reply: Germany, Spain, the Netherlands, Ireland, Portugal, Switzerland, Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Australia and Japan, among others.
  • Another number or a generic sender instead of yours, so their reply never reaches you: India (a random short number), France, Belgium, Finland, Poland, New Zealand, Mexico, Brazil, Singapore, South Africa and Israel, among others.
  • SMS from a foreign number aren't accepted, so the SMS shows Not delivered: the UK, Italy, Austria, the Philippines, the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia, among others.

The carriers change these rules from time to time.

Contacts in the US

A number outside the US can't send SMS to the US, Puerto Rico or the US Virgin Islands. The SMS is refused before it goes: US carriers don't accept texts from numbers outside the US. Text from a US number.

Send from a number in the contact's country

  • An SMS to a contact in your number's own country usually carries your number. Mexico is the exception: its carriers replace it too.
  • To reach a contact abroad as yourself and get their replies, send from a number in their country, where that country's numbers can send SMS.
  • New message suggests the number that contact last talked to, not one from their country, so check From before you send.

What it costs

An SMS is charged from your credits at the rate for the country it reaches, whichever number sends it.
