Agent questions.

What is an AI voice agent?

It's an assistant that answers your phone line in a natural voice. It talks with the caller, answers from the documents you give it, and books a callback or hands the call to your team when a person is needed.

When does it pick up?

You decide with call routing. Most teams send calls to the agent after hours, so nights and weekends are covered and your team keeps the day.

What can it answer?

Questions covered by the help docs, policies and FAQs you give it, in 30+ languages. You own those sources, so keep them current. If it doesn't know, it books a callback instead of guessing.

Will callers know it's AI?

Yes. The agent tells callers they're speaking with an automated assistant, and they can always ask for a person or leave a message.

How is it billed?

Per minute the agent spends on the call, at $0.12. Business includes 100 agent minutes per user each month and Scale includes 250, pooled across your account. Once a call is transferred to a person, the rest uses normal calling minutes.
