analytics and the wallboard, transfer, click to call, CRM logging, AI over call data (MCP, Ask AI), and running a
calling team.

100 rows: 35 point at rows already in `_blog/topics.md` ("see topics.md #N", counted toward the 100) and 65 are new.
Every row has at least one demand link. Links marked `ac` are Google autocomplete queries (US, English) run on
2026-11-18; the quoted text is a suggestion that query returned. Rows #2 and #25 point at section D of topics.md
(inbound): confirm with the inbound pillar which of you writes them, so they are not counted twice.
