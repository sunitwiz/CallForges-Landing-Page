# Voice agents: topic bank (researched 2026-11-18)

Pillar: the AI voice agent, inbound (AI receptionist, after-hours answering, qualifying, callbacks, FAQs,
hand-off to a person) and outbound (new-lead calls, callbacks, reminders, reactivation, surveys).

How to read this file:

- 100 rows, 5 phases of 20, ranked by demand inside each phase (strongest first).
- **ac** links are Google autocomplete (US English) for the phrase shown. Open the link to see the suggestions
  quoted in the Demand cell.
- Rows titled **see topics.md #N** are already planned in `_blog/topics.md`. Write them once, from that row;
  they are listed here only so this pillar's schedule has them in the right slot. 94 rows are new.
- **Facts from** uses the topics.md codes, plus `kb:<file>` for an entry in the app's `supportknowledge/`
  folder. `ask: outbound voice agent` = outbound facts must come from the owner (the feature ships today from
  another session). `ask` alone = no source has the app's labels yet (for example the AI agent's step name in a
  call workflow; `help:build-a-call-workflow` does not list one yet).
- **Legal** rows follow the topics.md legal rules: official sources linked, who the rule applies to, the
  "not legal advice" note, no verdict on CallForges' own calls.
- Sell only what is live. What the inbound agent does today (`ai-agent`, `kb:ai_agent`): answers 24/7 when
  routing sends it a call, answers only from the docs you give it, in 30+ languages, says it is an automated
  assistant at the start of every call, lets the caller reach a person or leave a message at any point, books a
  callback instead of guessing, $0.12 a minute (Business: 100 minutes per user pooled; Scale: 250), normal
  calling minutes after a transfer, 14-day trial with no card. Booking into a calendar is **not** documented:
  rows say "callback", never "books appointments".

## What people ask

- **"Will my callers just hang up on a bot?"** The most argued point. A 320-point Hacker News thread about an AI
  receptionist for a mechanic shop is mostly owners and customers saying they would hang up or call a rival; a
  30-day small-business test logged 22% of calls ending in the first 15 seconds, fewer when the agent said up
  front it was AI. [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47487536) ·
  [30-day test](https://www.lilachbullock.com/ai-phone-receptionist-30-day-test/) ·
  [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2026/aug/20/yorkshire-rotherham-ai-gp-receptionist-cannot-understand-accent)
- **"Can it understand accents, older callers and people with speech problems?"** UK GP surgeries pulled an AI
  receptionist after patients with broad Yorkshire accents and a stroke patient could not be understood; it
  became national news. [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2026/aug/20/yorkshire-rotherham-ai-gp-receptionist-cannot-understand-accent) ·
  [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/ai-gp-receptionist-stroke-patient-accessible-information-standard) ·
  [Futurism](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/patients-skipping-doctor-ai-receptionist)
- **"Will it make up prices or promises?"** Commenters doubt a bot can quote a job; the fix people ask for is an
  agent that admits what it does not know. [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47487536)
- **"What does it cost, per minute and per month, and is it worth it?"** "ai receptionist cost", "cost per
  month", "pricing", "ai voice agent cost per minute", "is ai receptionist worth it" and "roi calculator" are all
  top autocomplete completions. [ac](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=ai+receptionist+cost) ·
  [ac](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=ai+voice+agent+cost) ·
  [ac](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=is+ai+receptionist)
- **"Is it legal for AI to call people, and does it have to say it's AI?"** "are ai phone calls legal", "is ai
  cold calling legal in the us / uk", "tcpa ai", "ai disclosure law eu"; the FCC's 2024 AI-voice ruling drew a
  229-point HN thread. [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=39224249) ·
  [ac](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=is+ai+cold+calling+legal) ·
  [FCC 24-17](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-17A1.pdf)
- **"How fast do we have to call a new lead?"** "speed to lead meaning / statistics / harvard study / reddit".
  The source everyone cites is HBR 2011 (firms that replied within an hour were about 7 times more likely to
  qualify the lead; only 37% did). [HBR](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads) ·
  [ac](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=speed+to+lead)
- **"Why does it pause, or talk over me?"** Latency and turn-taking are the builders' obsession (570-point HN
  thread on a sub-500 ms agent; 113 points on noise and turn-taking); buyers feel it as "robotic".
  [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47224295) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43467988)
- **"How does an urgent caller reach a real person?"** Urgent calls that sound routine, and callers who want a
  human at once, are the second big objection. [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47487536) ·
  [LinkedIn](https://mx.linkedin.com/in/daviduribeh)
- **"Does it work for my trade?"** "ai receptionist for" fans out into dental, medical office, HVAC, plumbers,
  property management, roofing, law firms, med spas, auto repair, insurance.
  [ac](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=ai+receptionist+for) ·
  [ac](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=ai+receptionist+for+clinics)
- **"Should I just build my own?"** "how to build ai receptionist reddit", "ai phone agent github / open source";
  an open-source PBX voice agent drew 198 points on HN.
  [ac](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=how+to+set+up+ai+receptionist) ·
  [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46380399)

## Sources checked

What worked:

- **Google autocomplete**: about 140 phrases (US English, no personal data). The main demand signal, and the
  exact wording of the titles.
- **Hacker News** (Algolia API): 10 queries, plus 2 threads read in full. The strongest open discussion of
  AI receptionists, latency and the FCC ruling.
- **Web search**: about 30 queries for "people also ask" style questions, industry pains, legal sources and
  costs. Most results were vendor blogs. They were read for the questions only and are **not linked**, because
  their URLs carry vendor names.
- **News, studies and trade press**: the Guardian, The Next Web and Futurism on the UK GP rollout; HBR 2011 and
  the BYU record of it on lead response; PubMed Central on reminder calls; LawNext on law-firm call data; ACHR
  News on HVAC answering; Insurance Thought Leadership.
- **Official legal sources**: docs.fcc.gov (FCC 24-17, FCC 24-84), ecfr.gov (47 CFR 64.1200, 16 CFR 310.4,
  45 CFR 164.504), govinfo.gov (the revocation order), the EU AI Act service desk and EUR-Lex, and
  legislation.gov.uk (PECR reg. 19). All resolved. fcc.gov, hhs.gov, ico.org.uk, leginfo.legislature.ca.gov and
  the Commission's Article 50 FAQ refused a plain fetch (403/429), but appeared in search results, so they are
  linked.
- **Reference product**: its outbound voice agent setup guide and blog headings, for ideas only: setup steps,
  escalation rules, disclosure laws, how to test, CRM hookup, DNC checks, spam labels, metrics.

Blocked or limited:

- **Reddit, blocked three ways.** `old.reddit.com/.../search.json` was refused by the fetch tool, curl got 403,
  and web search refuses `reddit.com` as a domain filter. `site:reddit.com` queries returned no Reddit threads.
  - In its place: autocomplete shows people adding "reddit" to these searches ("ai receptionist reddit",
    "speed to lead reddit", "database reactivation reddit", "ai outbound calling agents reddit", "how to build
    ai receptionist reddit"). The demand is there; the threads could not be read.
- **LinkedIn**: the `site:linkedin.com/posts` and `/pulse` filters were not honoured. Only two public profile
  posts surfaced, and both are used as evidence.
- **Quora**: no pages returned.
- **G2 / Capterra**: review pages were seen, but every URL carries a vendor name, so none is linked. The cons
  seen there: some callers are put off once they realise it is AI, cost, call quality, caller ID problems.
- **YouTube**: reachable only through third-party summary pages whose URLs name vendors, so not linked.
  - The topics seen: no-code speed-to-lead builds, real-estate lead qualifiers, law-firm intake builds, "build
    and sell voice AI agents".
- **Home-services forum**: a field-service software community forum (two AI-receptionist threads) returned 403.
  Its snippet: AI is fine after hours and for routing, but weak on urgency and trust.
- **The reference product's AI voice agent landing page**: 404.
- **le.utah.gov**: timed out. Utah's SB 226 is cited from a law-firm summary, with the official URL to check.
