# Numbers and trust: topic bank (researched 2026-11-18)

Pillar: local numbers and area codes, toll-free vs local, buying numbers abroad, porting, answer rates and number
health, caller ID, calling rules (TCPA, DNC, state mini-TCPAs, calling hours, recording consent, UK PECR and TPS,
GDPR), business identity verification and spoofing.

Built around the live posts `what-is-stir-shaken`, `what-is-cnam` and `phone-number-reputation`: no row repeats
what they cover (attestation levels, CNAM set-up, label types, registering with the call-labelling companies,
disputing a label). Rows that say "see topics.md #N" are already planned there and count toward the 100.

Codes in **Facts from**: `help:<slug>` = `_help/src/articles/<slug>.html`; `kb:<file>` = the app's knowledge entry
in `backend/callforge_call_service/internal/supportknowledge/`; `intent:<file>` = `docs/intent/<file>` in the app
repo; `pricing`, `terms`, `fair-usage`, `auto-dialer`, `privacy` = those landing pages; `ask` = the app labels are
not written down anywhere yet, ask the user. **ac** links are search autocomplete results for that phrase
(US English), fetched on 2026-11-18.

Product facts that shape the angles (all live unless marked):
- One free local number per user in the US, UK, Canada or Australia; numbers in 56 countries; extra numbers from $5 a month.
- Local caller ID by country on Business and Scale.
- Porting: US local numbers through a wizard, other countries and US toll-free on request. $10 per US number, charged
  once the port date is set. The authorization e-mail comes from our carrier partner and must be signed within 30 days.
- Number verification once per country and number type. The US and Canada never ask. Address rules depend on the
  country and type (UK local: UK address; UK mobile: any country; German, French, Austrian, Irish, Mexican and
  Japanese local numbers: an address inside the number's area).
- Compliance center: the Business profile (free, any country, about 24 to 48 hours) gives level A signing. Without
  it, calls are signed at level B. Caller ID name and phone number reputation are also available.
- Number health on the Dashboard flags "May be labelled spam" from a drop in a number's pick-up rate.
- A workspace Do Not Call list. A contact's switch writes to it, a Settings page takes a CSV upload, manual dialing
  to a listed number is refused and campaigns skip it. Campaigns also have calling windows set by each lead's local time.
- Recording and transcripts can be switched on or off for each number, and turning on transcription shows a
  disclaimer. **Not live:** recording is not paused automatically on calls to all-party-consent states, so no post
  may claim it is.
- **Branded calling** (logo and call reason) is **coming soon only**.

## What people ask

- **"A brand-new number got flagged as spam on its first calls."** Fresh numbers are labelled almost at once, and
  answer rates collapse ([BiggerPockets: how to stop my cold calls saying I'm a scam](https://biggerpockets.com/forums/898/topics/1027401-how-to-stop-my-cold-calls-from-saying-im-a-scam),
  [BiggerPockets: number shows up as spam](https://biggerpockets.com/forums/93/topics/1107333-number-shows-up-as-spam),
  [insurance-forums: number getting flagged as spam](https://www.insurance-forums.com/community/threads/need-help-number-getting-flagged-as-spam.111115/post-1463492)).
- **"How many numbers do I need, and how often should I change them?"** Wholesalers and agents ask whether to churn
  numbers or use "burner" numbers ([do I need multiple numbers](https://biggerpockets.com/forums/48/topics/1156814-wholesale-calling-do-i-need-multiple-numbers-showing-as-spam),
  [how often do you need to change your phone](https://biggerpockets.com/forums/93/topics/1046819-when-cold-calling-how-often-do-you-need-to-change-your-phone),
  [burner numbers to cold call with](https://biggerpockets.com/forums/93/topics/1146141-best-service-for-burner-phone-numbers-to-cold-call-with)).
- **"My answer rate fell to almost nothing."** Reps notice the drop before they notice any label
  ([averaging about 1% answer rates](https://www.fishbowlapp.com/post/im-averaging-about-1-answer-rates-on-cold-calls-lately-and-its-driving-me-crazy-been-at-this-for-over-2-years-but-the-last),
  [low connect rate from UK to US](https://community.revgenius.com/x/chat-sales/lr5drsbsdh1r/low-call-connect-rate-from-uk-to-us-tips-and-data)).
- **"My port was rejected, or it has dragged on for weeks."** Names, account numbers or PINs that don't match, and
  losing carriers that stall ([10 reasons a port order may be rejected](https://www.nojitter.com/enterprise-networking/10-reasons-port-order-may-be-rejected),
  [a carrier rejecting ports for 4 months](https://puck.nether.net/pipermail/voiceops/2022-June/009593.html),
  [ac: port request rejected](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=port+request+rejected)).
- **"Why does buying a number abroad need my address?"** UK local numbers need a UK
  address and German local numbers need an address inside the area code
  ([ac: how to get a uk phone number](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=how+to+get+a+uk+phone+number),
  [Bundesnetzagentur numbering](https://bundesnetzagentur.de/EN/Areas/Telecommunications/Numbering/start.html)).
- **"Can I call business cell phones, FSBOs or expired listings?"** The questions are where the Do Not Call rules
  stop and whether B2B is exempt ([court: a business number is not always a business number](https://natlawreview.com/article/once-business-number-not-always-business-number-court-finds-shelton-can-sue-b2b),
  [NVAR on expired listings](https://nvar.com/services/laws/legal-blog/what-realtors-need-to-know-about-expired-or-cancelled-listings),
  [NAR on cold calling](https://www.nar.realtor/telemarketing-cold-calling)).
- **"State laws are stricter than the TCPA."** Florida and Oklahoma limit calls to 8am to 8pm and three calls in 24 hours
  ([Florida HB 761, 2023](https://flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2023/761/BillText/er/PDF),
  [Oklahoma HB 3168, 2022](https://oklegislature.gov/cf_pdf/2021-22%20ENR/hB/HB3168%20ENR.PDF),
  [15 O.S. 775C.4](https://law.justia.com/codes/oklahoma/title-15/section-15-775c-4/)).
- **"What are the rules for calling into the UK and Europe?"** The UK requires TPS and CTPS screening, France has
  required opt-in since 11 August 2026 and Germany requires consent ([ICO: telephone marketing](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guide-to-pecr/electronic-and-telephone-marketing/telephone-marketing/),
  [France's consent rule from 11 August 2026](https://www.leclubdesjuristes.com/?p=17147),
  [ac: is cold calling legal in europe](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=is+cold+calling+legal+in+europe)).
- **"Do I have to tell someone I'm recording the call?"** People ask this state by state
  ([ac: do i need to tell someone im recording a call](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=do+i+need+to+tell+someone+im+recording+a+call),
  [RCW 9.73.030](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=9.73.030)).
- **"Scammers are calling people from my number."** Businesses get angry callbacks for calls they never made
  ([local business gets 2,000 calls](https://www.eastidahonews.com/?p=352525),
  [FTC consumer comments](https://consumer.ftc.gov/comment/18266), [FCC: spoofing](https://fcc.gov/spoofing)).
