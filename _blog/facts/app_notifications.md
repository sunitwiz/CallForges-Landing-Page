# In the app: notifications, the bell, browser pop-ups and the daily e-mail (written from the app's own screens, 12 November 2026)

## Notifications: the bell notifies you when someone texts you, a conversation is assigned to you, or you missed a call or a voicemail
URL: https://callforges.com/settings#notifications-bell
Ask: Does the bell show a new text from a contact?
The bell at the top of the sidebar, beside the CallForges name, lists your missed calls, voicemails, new texts and the conversations assigned to you, and counts the ones you have not opened. It updates by itself while CallForges is open in a tab, with no refresh.
- A new text reads "New text from" the contact's name (or their number), then the start of their latest text, "3 new texts" when they sent more than one, the number they texted and when.
- More texts in the same conversation update that one row until you open it; the next text after that starts a new row.
- Who is told: the user the conversation is assigned to. When nobody is assigned, everyone who can see that number in the Inbox is told.
- Your own texts and the automatic out-of-hours reply never notify anyone.
- When another user assigns a text conversation to you, you get notified: the bell reads "Conversation assigned to you", with the contact's name (or their number) and who assigned it ("by Sam"). Clicking it opens the conversation in Messages → Inbox. Only you are told, not the rest of the workspace.
- You are not notified when you assign a conversation to yourself, when it is unassigned, or when it is assigned by itself (the first reply, round robin): the new text tells you then. Assigned to you again before you opened the first one, it stays one row.
- Clicking a text or an assignment opens the conversation in Messages → Inbox and marks it read. Opening the conversation in the Inbox marks its rows read in the bell too.
- Clicking a missed call or a voicemail opens the call in Call History.
- All shows everything, Unread only what you have not opened, and Mark all as read clears the count.

## Notifications settings: browser pop-ups and the daily e-mail
URL: https://callforges.com/settings#notifications
Ask: How do I turn on pop-ups for missed calls and texts?
Settings → Notifications has two switches of your own, both off until you turn them on.
- Browser pop-ups: a pop-up on your screen for each missed call, voicemail, text and conversation assigned to you, while CallForges is open in a tab. Your browser asks first; if you blocked it, allow notifications for CallForges in the browser's site settings.
- Daily e-mail: each morning at 8 AM in your profile's time zone, yesterday's missed calls and voicemails, sent to your sign-in e-mail. Texts and assigned conversations are not in it: answer them in the Inbox. It is not sent on a day with none.
- The bell itself is always on.
