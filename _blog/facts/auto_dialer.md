# Auto Dialer (from https://callforges.com/auto-dialer.html)

## What the auto dialer does
URL: https://callforges.com/auto-dialer.html
The CallForges auto dialer rings your list from a local number, inside each contact's working hours. You call one contact after another, or several at once, and the next call is already ringing when you hang up. A rep starts a session and the dialer rings each contact in turn; when the call ends, the rep logs the outcome and the next contact rings. There are 0 clicks between calls in power mode. Local numbers are available in 56 countries and territories.

## Power dialer and parallel dialer
URL: https://callforges.com/auto-dialer.html
- Power dialer: rings one contact at a time. The next contact rings as soon as the last call ends. If you reach voicemail, drop your recording in one click and keep going. Best for outreach blocks, event follow-ups and big lists.
- Parallel dialer: rings several contacts at the same time and connects you to the first person who answers. Best for large lists where most calls go unanswered.

## Dialer features and which plans have them
URL: https://callforges.com/auto-dialer.html
- Local caller ID: ring from a number in the contact's own country (Business and Scale).
- Calling windows: only dials inside each contact's working day (all plans).
- Voicemail drop: record it once, drop it in one click (all plans).
- Retry rules: missed contacts come back on a schedule (all plans; custom rules on Scale).
- AI summaries: a short summary of the call, synced to your CRM (Business and Scale).
- Recording and coaching: every call recorded on all plans; scorecards and wallboards on Scale.

## Dialer calls and free minutes
URL: https://callforges.com/auto-dialer.html
Auto dialer calls use minutes the same way as manual calls: calls to and from the US, Canada, UK and Australia use your plan's free minutes first, then bill per minute.
