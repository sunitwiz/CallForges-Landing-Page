# In the app: texting (SMS): registering numbers for texting (A2P 10DLC), and sending and receiving texts in Messages (written from the app's own screens and the texting contract, 12 November 2026)

## Register numbers for texting (A2P 10DLC)
URL: https://callforges.com/compliance/texting#register
Ask: How do I register numbers for texting?
US carriers block business texts from a local number that isn't registered. Registering is called A2P 10DLC: carriers review your business and a campaign, which says what you will text, to whom, and how people agree to get your texts. Once a campaign is approved, its numbers are registered for texting.
To register, open the Compliance center and find Text messaging (A2P 10DLC) below the Business profile. Click Add campaign. You need an approved Business profile first, and a role that can Buy and release numbers. A workspace can have up to 5 campaigns, and each number belongs to one campaign. Only US local and mobile numbers can be registered; toll-free and non-US numbers can't. Numbers outside the US don't use this registration: texting works in every country the carrier allows.
Registering costs credits: 4.5 credits once to register your business (plus 41.5 for business vetting, only if you'll send more than 2,000 texts a day), 15 credits, non-refundable, to review each campaign and again for each resubmission, charged when it goes to the carriers' review, then a monthly fee for each approved campaign.
Registered numbers send and receive texts in Messages. Your calls work as usual whether a number is registered or not. The icon beside Text messaging (A2P 10DLC) opens How registrations work on its Messaging tab, a diagram of these steps.

## Texting campaign steps: details, samples, user consent, description and review
URL: https://callforges.com/compliance/texting#campaign-steps
Ask: What does a texting campaign ask for?
Add campaign opens a form with its steps on the left:
- Campaign details: a campaign name, the use case (such as Customer care, Marketing or Account notifications; the fees for it show below), two to five sub use cases for a Mixed or Low volume mixed campaign, and the numbers. Only your US numbers in no other campaign are listed, and any number carriers refused. Your first campaign also asks "How many texts will you send a day?": Under 2,000 a day (the default) or More than 2,000 a day, which adds business vetting. Continue then asks you to accept the content carriers don't allow (such as cannabis, high-risk loans or gambling).
- Sample messages: two to five texts as your contacts will get them, 20 to 1,024 characters each. The first two are required; a third, fourth or fifth says (optional) and is left out when empty. Name your business in each and add "Reply STOP to opt out". Say whether your texts are about lending money, or contain links or phone numbers.
- User consent: how contacts agree to get your texts, 40 to 2,048 characters, such as a web form with its address. Opt-in keywords such as START, with the reply contacts get, are optional and go together.
- Description: what you text, to whom and why, 40 to 4,096 characters.
- Review and submit: every step read back with Edit beside it, and the carrier fees. Submit for review.
The form saves itself as a draft while you type and when you leave, so you can stop half-way; there is no Save draft button. Every field is required unless its name says (optional). Continue checks the step, and Submit for review checks every field and needs at least one number: an empty or wrong field turns red with a line under it saying what to enter. A field not accepted after Submit for review is outlined in red with a red ⓘ icon beside its name: hover over it, tap it or tab to it to read why, then change the field.

## Texting campaign statuses: a rejected campaign, resubmitting it, and deleting one
URL: https://callforges.com/compliance/texting#statuses
Ask: What do the texting campaign statuses mean?
Each campaign under Text messaging (A2P 10DLC) shows one status:
- Draft: saved, not sent. Continue it from the … menu on its row, or click the row. A draft the carriers sent back shows their reason under its name.
- In review: carriers are reviewing it, usually for 1 to 3 weeks, and it can't be changed until they answer. Statuses update by themselves; Refresh status checks now, at most once every 10 minutes.
- Approved: its numbers can be registered for texting. Add more numbers from View unregistered numbers or from a number's chips on Phone Numbers.
- Rejected: the carriers' reason shows in red under its name. Click Fix and resubmit, correct it and submit again. Each resubmission costs the review fee again, 15 credits, non-refundable, whatever you change.
Delete is the bin icon on each row, for any status. It withdraws the campaign from the carriers and stops its monthly fee; its numbers stop being registered for texting. You can delete a campaign even when texting isn't in your plan.

## A number carriers refused: add the refused number again or remove it
URL: https://callforges.com/compliance/texting#refused-numbers
Ask: Can I add a refused number again?
Carriers sometimes refuse one number of an approved campaign while they add the others. The campaign's page shows it as "refused" with their reason, and its texting chip on Phone Numbers shows the reason too. A refused number isn't held by its campaign: add it again, to the same campaign or another, by choosing it in a campaign's Numbers or with Add to campaign from its chip. To take it off the campaign instead, click Remove beside it on the campaign's page; this needs a role that can Buy and release numbers. Only a refused number can be removed on its own.

## Texting registration deleted when your plan ends: how to keep it
URL: https://callforges.com/compliance/texting#plan-ends
Ask: Is the texting registration deleted when the plan ends?
Yes, when the grace after your plan ends runs out. When a plan ends (the free trial expired, you cancelled, or the workspace was suspended for an unpaid invoice), Phone Numbers and Billing show the date in red: "Your numbers will be released on <date>, and your texting registration deleted with them", or "Your texting registration will be deleted on <date>" when no number is released. On that date your numbers go back to the carrier and every texting campaign is deleted, which stops its monthly fee. Registering again takes a new campaign review of 15 credits, non-refundable, and 1 to 3 weeks. Choose a plan before that date (for a suspended workspace, pay the open invoice) to keep both your numbers and your texting registration.

## Texting registration fees and charges: business registration (brand) fee, when it is charged, campaign review fee (non-refundable), resubmit fee, monthly fee, credits to submit
URL: https://callforges.com/compliance/texting#fees
Ask: Is there a fee to register for texting?
Yes. Unlike the Business profile, texting carries the carriers' own fees, charged to your credits:
- Registering your business (brand) with US carriers: once per workspace, with your first campaign (4.5 credits today), charged at once when carriers receive your business registration, right after you submit. If carriers never receive it, nothing is charged.
- Business vetting: once, only if you chose More than 2,000 texts a day on your first campaign (41.5 credits today), charged together with the business registration.
- Campaign review: charged when the campaign goes to the carriers' review, once for each new campaign and again each time you resubmit a rejected one (15 credits today, non-refundable). If carriers reject your business registration, the campaigns waiting for it are never charged the review.
- A monthly fee for each approved campaign until you delete it, by use case (10 credits a month today, 2.4 for Low volume mixed).
Review shows each fee and the total due now before you submit. Submit for review needs credits that cover what is due now; if they don't, Review says so, with Add credits for a role that can add them (others are asked to ask the workspace owner). Resubmitting a rejected campaign costs the review fee again, 15 credits, non-refundable. Deleting a campaign stops its monthly fee.

## Business vetting for texting: do I need vetting, the 2,000 texts a day limit
URL: https://callforges.com/compliance/texting#vetting
Ask: Do I need business vetting for texting?
Most businesses don't. Your first campaign asks "How many texts will you send a day?". Under 2,000 a day, the default, registers your business without vetting, for 4.5 credits. More than 2,000 a day adds business vetting, 41.5 credits once: carriers have an independent firm check your business and give it a trust score, which sets how many texts you can send a day. Without vetting, carriers let a business send up to about 2,000 texts a day, so choose vetting only if you'll send more than that. The question is asked once, while your business isn't registered yet; later campaigns don't ask.

## Texting in the free trial: can I text on a trial
URL: https://callforges.com/compliance/texting#trial
Ask: Is texting included in the free trial?
Partly. Texting works on a trial from a number in any country that needs no carrier registration — the UK, Australia, Germany, France and the rest — paying only for the texts you send with your credits. Texting is not included in the free trial for a number in the US, Puerto Rico, the US Virgin Islands or Canada: those need 10DLC registration, whose carrier fees a trial doesn't include. The Inbox shows "Numbers in the US and Canada need 10DLC registration, which the trial doesn't include." beside such a number, and texting from it stays off, and New message is off while it is the number you are on. Choose a plan and you can register, with credits that cover the fees due when you submit. If texting shows "Not included in your plan" instead, it was turned off for your workspace: contact support to turn it on, which turns it on for every country.

## Send a text message (SMS) to a contact from Messages
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#send
Ask: How do I send a text message?
To send a text (an SMS, or "txt") to a contact or lead, open Inbox (under Messaging in the sidebar), open the contact's conversation or click New message and pick the contact or type their number, write your text and click Send. It goes from one of your numbers, and the contact's replies come back to the same conversation. You text from the numbers you may call from.
Which numbers can text:
- US numbers: only once they are in an approved texting campaign (see Register numbers for texting).
- Toll-free numbers and voice-only numbers can't text.
- Numbers in other countries: texting works in every country the carrier allows. A number outside the US can't text a US contact: US carriers don't accept those texts.
Sending needs credits: with 0 credits, a text is refused until you add credits. A contact who replied STOP can't be texted until they reply START.

## Texting a contact in another country: SMS to India or France from a foreign number, an Indian contact sees a code, what number they see, and whether they can reply
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#other-countries
Ask: Can I text a contact in another country, such as India from a UK number?
In most countries, India included. The carriers in the contact's country decide whether the text is accepted and whether your number shows, by that country's rules:
- Your number appears, and the contact can reply: Germany, Spain, the Netherlands, Ireland, Portugal, Switzerland, Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Australia and Japan, among others.
- Another number or a generic sender appears instead of yours, so the contact's reply never reaches you: India (the contact sees a code or a random short sender, not your number), France, Belgium, Finland, Poland, New Zealand, Mexico, Brazil, Singapore, South Africa and Israel, among others.
- Texts from a foreign number aren't accepted, so the text is marked Not delivered: the UK, Italy, Austria, the Philippines, the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia, among others.
- The US, Puerto Rico and the US Virgin Islands: a number outside the US can't text there, and the text is refused before it goes: "US carriers don't accept texts from numbers outside the US. Text from a US number."
A text to a contact in your number's own country usually carries your number (not in Mexico, where carriers replace it too). To text a contact abroad as yourself and get their replies, text them from a number in their country, where that country's numbers can text. A text is charged at the price of the country it reaches. Carriers change these rules from time to time.

## Which countries' numbers can text: German, French, Canadian and other numbers, US registration, and Indian numbers
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#countries
Ask: Which countries' numbers can send texts?
A number texts when the carrier lets it (mobile numbers, and local numbers in the US and Canada) and its country is one of these:
- Sending and receiving, with no registration: Australia, Austria, Belgium, Brazil, Canada, Chile, the Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Hong Kong, Hungary, Israel, Italy, Jersey, Lithuania, Mexico, Myanmar, the Netherlands, Norway, the Philippines, Poland, Portugal, South Africa, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Thailand and the UK.
- The US, Puerto Rico and the US Virgin Islands: once the number is registered for texting (A2P 10DLC).
- A number you hold from India, Malaysia or Singapore can't send texts yet: their carriers need a registration CallForges doesn't offer yet.
- Numbers from every other country, such as Ireland, Japan or Iceland, can't text.
Toll-free numbers and most landline numbers can't text. A number you ported in texts by the same rules as one you bought. On Phone Numbers, each number that can text has the SMS chip, and the Inbox says why a number can't.

## How many characters in one text: 160, or 70 with emoji or other alphabets, max length 1,600 characters, and long texts sent in parts
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#length
Ask: How many characters can one text have?
One text holds 160 characters. With an emoji, or letters outside the standard texting alphabet (such as Chinese, Arabic, Hindi or some accented letters), it holds 70. A longer text goes out in parts that the contact's phone joins into one message: 153 characters a part, or 67 with an emoji or another alphabet, and each part is charged as one text. A text can be up to 1,600 characters and 10 parts, your signature included. The counter under the message box counts how many texts it goes out as and the characters used, for example "2 texts · 161 / 306".

## Send pictures (MMS) in a text, and pictures or attachments contacts send you
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#pictures
Ask: Can I send a picture in a text?
Not yet: the texts you send are text only. Pictures a contact sends you do arrive: their text lists Attachment 1, Attachment 2 and so on under it, and clicking one opens it in a new tab. The MMS chip on Phone Numbers marks the numbers the carrier lets send and receive pictures.

## Bulk SMS: one message to many contacts at once (mass SMS or an SMS blast)
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#bulk-texting
Ask: Can I send a text to many contacts at once?
Not yet: each text goes to one contact. Start each one from New message, or schedule it to go later. The Inbox's bulk actions close, reopen, archive or mark many conversations at once, but they don't send texts.

## Texting at night: quiet hours, and what time texts go out
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#quiet-hours
Ask: Does CallForges stop texts at night?
No. A text goes out when you send it, or at the time you scheduled it, whatever the hour where the contact is. Some countries limit marketing texts to certain hours or days, such as Italy, Brazil and South Africa, so check the rules of your contacts' country, and schedule texts for their daytime.

## Receive texts and replies: customers and contacts text you back
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#receive
Ask: Can contacts text our numbers back?
Yes. When a contact replies to your text, or texts one of your numbers that can text, the text lands in your Inbox, in that contact's conversation for that number, and shows as unread. A closed or archived conversation reopens when the contact texts again. Received texts are charged like sent ones; see what a text costs.

## Text delivery status: what a text says (Sending, Sent, Delivered, Not delivered, Not confirmed yet or unconfirmed), and Try again
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#status
Ask: What does Not delivered or Not confirmed yet mean on a text?
Under each text you send, the Inbox shows where it is:
- Sending: it is on its way.
- Sent: the carrier took it, and its delivery isn't reported yet.
- Delivered: it reached the contact's phone.
- Not delivered: it didn't arrive, and the reason shows under it, for example the phone is off, a landline, a number that no longer exists, or a contact who replied STOP. Click Try again to send it once more, from the same number. Each text can be sent again once.
- Not confirmed yet (unconfirmed): the carrier didn't answer as the text was sent, so we're checking with the carrier whether it went out. It is never sent twice. Within about 10 minutes it shows what really happened, or turns to Not delivered with "We couldn't confirm this text was sent. Check with the contact before you try again."
- Scheduled: it goes out at the time shown under it. Until then it can be edited or cancelled; see schedule a text.

## Messages inbox: where to see your texts, and who sees which numbers
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#inbox
Ask: Where do I see the texts?
Inbox, under Messaging in the sidebar, is where your texts are. Each conversation holds every text between one contact and one of your numbers, sent and received, so a contact who texts two of your numbers has two conversations. The unread count beside Inbox shows how many texts are still unread. Pick a number at the top to see its conversations and whether it can text.
Each conversation in the list shows your draft ("Draft:") if you started one, otherwise its last text. Calls aren't in the Inbox: a missed call shows in the bell of each user it rang (when it rang nobody, of the user it was for), and every call is in Call History.
You see the texts of the numbers you may call from, and text from them. A user whose role has "Read the texts of every number in the workspace" sees every number's texts, a colleague's numbers included.

## Inbox labels: Opted out, Not delivered, the unread dot; write a long text in a bigger box (Expand opens a larger view); the keyboard shortcut to send a text (Ctrl or ⌘ + Enter)
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#labels
Ask: What do the labels in the Inbox mean?
Each conversation in the Inbox list can carry a label under its last text:
- Opted out: the contact replied STOP, so they can't be texted until they reply START.
- Not delivered (in red, with a red dot on the contact's picture): your last text didn't arrive. Open the conversation to see why and click Try again.
A blue dot beside the name and the time in blue mean the conversation has unread texts.
To read or write in a larger view, click Expand (the arrows beside Send): the whole conversation opens large, and what you wrote stays when you close it. Press Ctrl + Enter (⌘ + Enter on a Mac) to send; Enter starts a new line.

## Text conversations: assign, close, reopen, archive, mark read or unread
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#conversations
Ask: How do I assign or close a text conversation?
In a conversation in the Inbox you can:
- Assign it to a user, or leave it unassigned. The user you assign it to sees it in their bell, unless you assign it to yourself.
- Close it when it's done, and reopen it later. A closed conversation also reopens when the contact texts again.
- Archive it to put it away, and unarchive it later; see archive or close.
- Mark it read or unread.
To do one of these to several conversations at once, see bulk actions. Who gets a conversation by itself is set in Settings → Messaging settings → Messaging, under Conversations.

## Archive a conversation: the difference between archive and close, and unarchive
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#archive
Ask: What's the difference between archive and close?
Close a conversation when it's done: it moves to the Closed tab, and you can reopen it. Archive one to put it away for good, such as spam or a contact you won't text again: it leaves Open and Closed for the Archived tab. You can archive an open or a closed conversation.
Both come back by themselves: when the contact texts again, a closed or archived conversation reopens in Open. To bring an archived one back yourself, click Unarchive; it opens again. An archived conversation can't be closed or reopened until you unarchive it.
While "Unassign when a conversation closes" is on, closing or archiving a conversation clears its assignee. A number with "Archive the conversation when the contact opts out" on archives the contact's conversation on it when they reply STOP.

## Bulk actions on text conversations: close, reopen, archive, unarchive, mark read or unread many at once
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#bulk
Ask: Can I archive or close many text conversations at once?
Yes. In the Inbox list, tick the conversations you want, up to 100 at a time, then choose Close, Reopen, Archive, Unarchive, Mark read or Mark unread. Each conversation is changed as if you had done it on its own, so one that can't change doesn't stop the others: an archived conversation isn't closed or reopened (unarchive it first), and one you can no longer see is left as it is. You're told how many changed and which didn't, with the reason.

## Filter text conversations by replies: with replies or without replies (no reply yet)
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#replies
Ask: How do I see conversations with no reply?
The Replies filter in the Inbox shows only the conversations where the contact has texted you at least once (With replies), or only those where they never have (Without replies), such as texts you sent that got no answer. It works with every tab and the other filters.

## Drafts: where are my drafts, a text you started but didn't send
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#drafts
Ask: Where are drafts kept in Messages?
What you write in a conversation and don't send is kept as your draft, so it's still there when you come back, on any device. The Drafts tab in the Inbox lists the conversations where you have a draft. Your drafts are yours alone: other users don't see them. Sending the text, or scheduling it, deletes the draft, and so does clearing the box. You see a draft only while you can still see its conversation.

## Schedule a text to send later: pick a date and time, see, edit or cancel a scheduled text
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#schedule
Ask: How do I schedule a text?
To send a text later, such as tomorrow morning, write it and choose Schedule beside Send, then pick the date and time: at least 2 minutes from now and at most a year ahead. The text shows in the conversation with its time, marked Scheduled, and the Scheduled tab lists the conversations that have one. A text scheduled to a contact you haven't texted from that number yet is in the Scheduled tab only, not in Open, until it goes out; cancelling it removes that new conversation.
Until it goes out, the user who scheduled it, or a user who can manage texting settings, can edit its text and time or cancel it. Once it is being sent it can't be changed.
At its time it goes out from the same number, with the signature of the user who scheduled it. It is checked again then, as any text is: if the contact replied STOP, the number can no longer text, that user can no longer text from the number, or your credits have run out, it isn't sent and shows Not delivered with the reason. Credits are charged only for a text that goes out. Whether the contact or the number can text is also checked when you schedule it, so a contact who already replied STOP can't be scheduled.

## Who gets a new text conversation: remain assigned, first response or round robin, and the assignee when a conversation closes or the contact opts out
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#texting-conversations
Ask: Who gets a new text conversation?
In Settings → Messaging settings → Messaging, Conversations sets who a text conversation is assigned to, the same for every number in the workspace.
New conversations, when a contact texts you first:
- Remain assigned (the default): the conversation waits unassigned until a user replies, then stays with whoever replied last. When another user replies, it moves to them.
- First response: the conversation waits unassigned until a user replies, and the first user who replies keeps it.
- Round robin: the conversation goes at once to the number's users in turn. Replies don't move it.
Three switches, all on by default:
- Assign a conversation to whoever starts it: when you text a contact from New message, the conversation is yours. Off, it stays unassigned.
- Unassign when a conversation closes: closing or archiving a conversation clears its assignee. When the contact texts again it reopens, still unassigned.
- Unassign when the contact opts out: when a contact replies STOP, their conversations on every number are unassigned.
Anyone who can read texts sees these settings; changing them needs the permission to manage texting settings.

## A contact replied STOP: when contacts reply STOP, text opt-outs, START to text them again, and HELP
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#opt-outs
Ask: What happens when a contact replies STOP?
A contact who replies STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL or a similar word opts out (unsubscribes) of your texts: from then on a text to them is refused, from any number in your workspace. When they reply START, you can text them again. On a number outside the US and Canada, a reply counts only when the whole text is one such word ("Stop." counts, "please stop texting me" doesn't), and no confirmation text goes back to the contact. A contact who replies HELP isn't opted out: their text lands in the Inbox like any other, so answer it there.
Their conversations are unassigned on every number while "Unassign when the contact opts out" is on (it is by default). The conversation on a number is also archived when that number has "Archive the conversation when the contact opts out" on. Both are in Settings → Messaging settings → Messaging.

## Out-of-hours reply for a number (away message): opening hours, how often it's sent, and archive on opt-out
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#texting-number
Ask: How do I set an out-of-hours reply for a number?
Each number has its own out-of-hours automatic reply. In Settings → Messaging settings → Messaging, pick the number under Out-of-hours reply, turn the reply on, and set the number's opening hours, their time zone and the reply's text (up to 320 characters). The number's own signature is added to it, and the two together must fit in one text of up to 10 parts.
How often it's sent: a contact who texts the number while it's closed gets the reply at most once every 24 hours from that number, however many texts they send. Contacts who replied STOP never get it, and a text that only says STOP, START or HELP gets no reply. If texting is unavailable when their text arrives, the reply is tried again until it goes; 24 hours after their text it's marked Not sent instead.
Archive on opt-out: turn on "Archive the conversation when the contact opts out" for a number, and when a contact replies STOP to that number, their conversation on it is archived.

## Saved replies (text templates)
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#saved-replies
Ask: How do I save a reply to reuse in texts?
Saved replies are texts you write once, with a name, in Settings → Messaging settings → Saved replies, and insert in any conversation from Saved replies under the message box.

## Text signatures: a signature for each user, for one number, or for a user on a number, which signature is used, and the 160 characters
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#texting-signatures
Ask: Can each user have their own signature?
A signature is added to the end of the texts your users send. Set signatures in Settings → Messaging settings → Messaging, under Signatures. Each one has a name, a text of up to 160 characters, and where it applies:
- one user on one number;
- one user on every number: each user can have their own signature;
- one number, for everyone who texts from it: you can set a different signature for each number.
One signature is on at a time for each of these; turning one on turns the earlier one off.
Which signature is used when several apply: only one is added to a text. A signature for the user on that number wins, then the user's own, then the number's. The out-of-hours reply carries the number's signature.
Does the signature count toward the 160 characters? Yes. The signature goes on a new line at the end of the text, so it counts toward the text's length and the number of texts it's sent as, up to 10.
A user who can manage texting settings manages every signature. Others add, edit and delete only their own, on the numbers they can text from, and see those numbers' signatures.

## Text message cost: what each text is charged, and the fee for failed texts
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#cost
Ask: How much does a text message cost?
Each text (SMS), sent or received, is charged from your credits. A long text goes out as several texts, each one charged: the counter under the message box shows how many, for example "2 texts · 161 / 306". Sending needs credits: with 0 credits, a text is refused until you add credits. Any extra fee added beyond a text's own price, such as the small fee for a text that failed, is charged from your credits the same way, about two days later, and nothing is ever charged twice. Registering numbers for texting has its own fees; see texting registration fees.

## AI tools (MCP) and texting: an AI assistant reads and replies to your texts, sends or schedules SMS, and assigns, closes or archives conversations
URL: https://callforges.com/messages#ai-tools
Ask: Can an AI tool send and schedule texts?
Yes. An AI tool you connect in Settings → MCP access works in Messages as you, and only as far as your role allows. It can:
- Read your texting numbers and whether each one can text, your conversations and their texts, your texting settings, the texting registration and your texting stats, and suggest which of your numbers to text a contact from.
- Send a text (an SMS, or "txt") or reply in a conversation, now or scheduled for later, from a number you may text from.
- Assign a conversation, and close, reopen, archive, unarchive or mark conversations read or unread.
Before it sends, the tool shows you the number the text goes from, who it goes to, the exact text with your signature and how many texts it counts as, and it is told to send only after you say yes. It picks the number the way New message does; when none of your numbers fits that contact, it asks you which number to text from. In Ask AI you always confirm a text with Confirm. A tool that retries a send never sends the same text twice, and your own unsent draft in that conversation stays.
It cannot cancel or edit a scheduled text, or change texting settings, saved replies, signatures or the texting registration: do those in the app.
What your role needs, in Settings → Roles & permissions:
- Reading texts needs "Read the texts of the numbers you may call from" (under Texting). The texting registration also needs "View numbers", and the texting stats "View call logs".
- Sending, scheduling, assigning, closing, archiving and marking need "Send texts and assign, close, reopen and mark conversations", and a key or connected app with Read & write access. A Read only key or app reads but never sends.
- Connecting the tool at all needs "Connect AI tools (MCP)".
Ask AI, the assistant inside the app, does the same on the Messages page with no key and no MCP access, as far as your role allows: it reads your conversations and prepares a text, an assignment or a close as a card, and nothing happens until you press Confirm.
A text an AI tool sends is a text like any other: it costs credits like a text you send, with 0 credits it is refused, and the same checks apply, so a contact who replied STOP is never texted until they reply START. Under a text an AI tool sent, the Inbox shows MCP and "Sent through" with the tool and its key or app, or "Scheduled through" while the text waits to go out; a text Ask AI sends shows "Sent through Ask AI · in the app". In Settings → Activity each one shows with the MCP badge as Text sent, Text scheduled or Text not sent (the carrier refused it at once, so nothing went out), and the conversations it assigns, closes or archives show there too.
