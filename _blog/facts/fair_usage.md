# Fair Usage Policy (from https://callforges.com/fair-usage.html, last updated 10 October 2026)

## Included minutes
URL: https://callforges.com/fair-usage.html#included
The Fair Usage Policy explains how calling minutes work and what counts as normal business use; it is part of the Terms and Conditions, and the Terms apply if the two conflict. Every user licence includes free calling minutes each billing month for calls to and from phone numbers in the United States, Canada, United Kingdom and Australia:
- Free trial: 50 inbound and 50 outbound minutes. Each call can last up to 5 minutes until you add a card. With a card, you can take up to 5 numbers per workspace.
- Starter: 100 minutes per user per month.
- Business: 500 minutes per user per month.
- Scale: 1,000 minutes per user per month.
The allowance is calculated at account level: your plan's minutes multiplied by the number of active paid users in that billing month. Inbound and outbound calls both use the allowance. Free minutes reset at the start of each billing month; unused minutes do not roll over and have no cash value.

## How minutes are counted
URL: https://callforges.com/fair-usage.html#counting
- Calls are billed per minute. Any part of a minute is rounded up to the next full minute.
- Each connected call, including a call answered by voicemail, counts as at least 1 minute.
- Calls that do not connect (no answer, busy, invalid number) are not billed.
- Forwarded calls use minutes for each leg of the call.

## After your free minutes
URL: https://callforges.com/fair-usage.html#after
Once your account uses its monthly allowance, further calls to and from the US, Canada, UK and Australia are billed per minute at the rates published on the pricing page. Calls to every other country, and to premium-rate, special-service or satellite numbers, are always billed per minute and never use the free allowance. Each call's cost shows in Billing after the call. Minutes beyond your allowance, calls to and from other countries, call transcripts, AI call summaries and the porting fee are paid from your credits. Number rent is charged to your card with each renewal. You can top up credits once you subscribe, or during the free trial once you add a card.

## AI agent and transcription minutes
URL: https://callforges.com/fair-usage.html#agent
AI voice agent minutes are separate from calling minutes. Business plans include 100 agent minutes per user per month and Scale plans include 250, calculated at account level. Additional agent minutes are billed per minute at the published rate. When the agent transfers a call to a person, the rest of the call uses normal calling minutes. Call transcription is billed separately, per transcribed minute, at the published rate; transcribed minutes do not use your calling allowance.

## Normal business use, and what isn't allowed
URL: https://callforges.com/fair-usage.html#not-allowed
Minutes are included for the named user who holds the licence, making and receiving calls as part of their normal work: sales calls, follow-ups, customer support and similar conversations with real people. The free allowance, and CallForges generally, may not be used for:
- Sharing one licence between several people, or rotating logins to spread usage.
- Reselling minutes or numbers, or connecting CallForges to another phone system, PBX, SIP trunk or call-center platform so that many people use one licence.
- Automated, machine-to-machine or pre-recorded calls without the consent the law requires.
- Traffic pumping, SIM boxing, call arbitrage, or calls made mainly to generate minutes or revenue.
- Calls that break the Acceptable Use rules in the Terms and Conditions.

## Auto dialer use under the policy
URL: https://callforges.com/fair-usage.html#dialer
Auto dialer calls use minutes the same way as manual calls. Because the dialer places calls quickly, CallForges watches for patterns that suggest misuse, such as very short average call length, very high no-answer rates, or large numbers of calls to numbers on do-not-call registers. You are responsible for calling hours, consent and do-not-call rules in each country you call (section 9 of the Terms and Conditions).

## Monitoring and enforcement
URL: https://callforges.com/fair-usage.html#enforcement
CallForges reviews usage to protect its network, its carriers and other customers. If it sees usage that breaks the policy it may, in this order where practical: contact you to explain what it found and ask you to change it; bill the affected minutes at the published per-minute rates; limit calling volume, disable the auto dialer or pause specific numbers; suspend or close the account as described in the Terms. Where the law or a carrier requires it to act at once, for example to stop fraud or illegal calling, it may act without notice.

## Changes to the policy and contact
URL: https://callforges.com/fair-usage.html#contact
CallForges may update the policy as its plans and carrier costs change, and gives at least 30 days' notice by e-mail or in the app before a change that reduces your included minutes takes effect. Questions about the policy go to support@callforges.com.
