# Pricing (from https://callforges.com/pricing.html)

## Plans and prices
URL: https://callforges.com/pricing.html
CallForges has three plans, priced per user per month. Annual billing saves up to 26%.
- Starter: $15 per user a month billed annually, or $19 billed monthly. The essentials for small teams calling across borders. 100 free minutes per user per month.
- Business (most popular): $29 per user a month billed annually, or $39 billed monthly. For sales and support teams that dial every day. 500 free minutes per user per month.
- Scale: $45 per user a month billed annually, or $59 billed monthly. For high-volume teams and multi-country operations. 1,000 free minutes per user per month.
On every plan the free minutes cover inbound and outbound calls to and from the US, UK, Canada and Australia. There is no seat minimum: you can start with one seat.

## What the Starter plan includes
URL: https://callforges.com/pricing.html
- 1 free local US, UK, Canada or Australia number per user

## Large teams and volume rates
URL: https://callforges.com/pricing.html
Teams with 50+ seats or a contact-center setup can talk to CallForges about volume rates, dedicated numbers and custom SLAs by booking a demo from the pricing page.

## Free minutes and international calling
URL: https://callforges.com/pricing.html#rates
Every user gets free minutes each month for calls to and from the US, UK, Australia and Canada: 100 on Starter, 500 on Business and 1,000 on Scale. After that, and for every other country, you pay per minute, and each call's cost shows in Billing after the call. Calls to the US, UK, Australia and Canada (landline and mobile) are included up to the plan's free minutes, then billed per minute. The rate table on the pricing page currently shows sample per-minute rates for other countries, shown for layout; final rates publish at launch.

## Add-ons: extra numbers, toll-free, AI agent minutes, minutes beyond the pool, transcription
URL: https://callforges.com/pricing.html
- Extra number: from $5 a month. Numbers in 56 countries and territories, per user or shared by a team. The price depends on the country and the number type.
- Toll-free number: from $5 a month. A free-to-call line for support desks: $5 in the US, Canada and UK, $25 in Australia, more in other countries.
- AI voice agent minutes: $0.12 a minute for the minutes the AI agent spends on calls beyond the plan's pool. Transfers to your team aren't counted.
- Minutes beyond your pool: billed per minute at the published rate for each destination and paid from your credits. There are no bundles to buy.
- Call transcription: billed per transcribed minute at the published rate.

## Credits (top up as you go)
URL: https://callforges.com/pricing.html
Credits pay for minutes after your free pool, calls to and from other countries, call transcripts, AI call summaries and the porting fee. Number rent goes on your card with each renewal. You can top up credits once you subscribe, or during the free trial once you add a card.

## Seat minimum and mixing plans
URL: https://callforges.com/pricing.html
There is no seat minimum: start with one seat and add people when you need them. You can't mix plans: your workspace has one plan and every user is on it, so a plan change applies to everyone.

## How minutes are counted and whether they roll over
URL: https://callforges.com/pricing.html
Minutes are counted per minute, rounded up to the next full minute. Calls to and from the US, UK, Australia and Canada use your plan's free minutes first. Unused free minutes do not roll over: they reset at the start of each billing month.

## How the AI voice agent is billed
URL: https://callforges.com/pricing.html
Per minute the agent spends talking, at $0.12. Business and Scale include a pool of agent minutes each month (100 and 250 per user). Time after a transfer to a person counts as a normal call.

## How transcription is billed
URL: https://callforges.com/pricing.html
Call transcription is billed per transcribed minute, at the published rate.

## Porting numbers
URL: https://callforges.com/pricing.html
You can port your numbers, including during your free trial. Porting a US number costs $10. Fees for other countries vary by carrier.

## Plan changes, proration and cancelling
URL: https://callforges.com/pricing.html
Upgrades and added seats are prorated: you pay only for the rest of the current billing period, up to your renewal date. A downgrade starts at your renewal. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime. Annual plans can change or downgrade at renewal.
