# The free trial (from https://callforges.com/pricing.html and https://callforges.com/terms.html#trial)

## What the free trial includes
URL: https://callforges.com/pricing.html
The free trial lasts 14 days, and no card is needed to start. You get a free local US, UK, Canada or Australia number, 50 inbound and 50 outbound minutes, and calls of up to 5 minutes each. Adding a card removes the 5-minute limit, lets you take up to 5 numbers per workspace and lets you top up credits. Campaigns (the power and parallel dialers) start with your plan. You can port your numbers during the trial. If you don't choose a plan, the trial number is released on a later date, shown on the Phone Numbers page.

## Trial rules in the Terms
URL: https://callforges.com/terms.html#trial
Free trials run for 14 days unless CallForges says otherwise, and CallForges may extend a trial. During a trial you get one free local number in the US, UK, Canada or Australia, 50 inbound and 50 outbound minutes, and calls of up to 5 minutes each. A payment card is required for any number after the first. Adding a card removes the 5-minute call limit, lets you take up to 5 numbers per workspace and lets you buy credits. At the end of the trial the account stops unless you choose a paid plan, and the trial number is released on a later date, shown on the Phone Numbers page. CallForges does not charge you during a trial without your agreement.

## Which calls the trial minutes cover
URL: https://callforges.com/fair-usage.html#included
The trial's 50 inbound and 50 outbound minutes are free calling minutes for calls to and from phone numbers in the United States, Canada, United Kingdom and Australia. Each call can last up to 5 minutes until you add a card. With a card, you can take up to 5 numbers per workspace, and you can top up credits.
