# In the app: the Ask AI panel itself (written from the app's own screens, 14 November 2026)

## Ask AI panel: keep it open, minimize it, expand it, and find past conversations
URL: https://callforges.com/app#ask-ai
Ask: How does Ask AI minimize, and how does a conversation come back?
The Ask AI button beside the bell at the top of the sidebar opens Ask AI on the right of the screen. It stays open as you go: clicking the page or moving to another page in the sidebar keeps it and your conversation.
- On a wide screen the page makes room for it; on a smaller one it lies over the right of the page.
- Drag its left edge to make it wider or narrower; CallForges remembers the width on this browser.
- Minimize (the – in its header) shrinks it to a small bar at the bottom right with your conversation's title. Click the bar, or the Ask AI button, to bring it back.
- Expand (the arrows in its header) spreads it over the page, with your past conversations on the left. Collapse brings it back to the side, and so does moving to another page.
- ✕ closes it. Your conversation is kept: Ask AI opens on it again.
- Past conversations: click the conversation's title at the top of the panel to see them under Today and Older, search them, open one, or start a New conversation. The + starts a new one too. They are kept on this browser for you and this workspace, the latest 20, and cleared when you log out.
- Opening Settings or another window moves Ask AI aside; it comes back when you close it.
