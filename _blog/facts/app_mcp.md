# In the app: AI tools (MCP access), connecting an AI assistant to your workspace (written from the app's own screens and the MCP contract, 13 November 2026)

## AI tools (MCP access): connect an AI assistant or AI app to your workspace, with a key or a connected app, read only or read & write
URL: https://callforges.com/settings#mcp
Ask: How do I connect an AI tool to the workspace?
In Settings → MCP access you connect AI tools, such as AI assistants, AI chat apps and code editors, to this workspace. A tool works as you: it can only do what your role allows, and a change to your role applies to it at once. You need "Connect AI tools (MCP)" in Settings → Roles & permissions, under AI tools (MCP). AI tools are not included in the free trial.
Two ways to connect:
- Apps that sign in: in the AI app, add a custom connector, paste the Server URL, then sign in with CallForges and pick this workspace. The app shows under Connected apps; click Disconnect to cut it off.
- Tools that use a key: click Create key, name it, pick its access and when it expires (30 days, 90 days, 1 year or Never), and copy the key at once: you won't see it again. Use it with the Key URL. A workspace can have up to 25 keys; click Revoke to stop one.
Access is Read only or Read & write. Read only sees your calls, contacts, campaigns, texts and more. Read & write can also create and edit contacts, lists, campaigns and workflow drafts, publish workflows, and send texts and work your Inbox; see AI tools and texting.
Changes an AI tool makes are recorded in Settings → Activity with an MCP badge and the tool's name, and a text it sends shows "Sent through" the tool in the Inbox.
