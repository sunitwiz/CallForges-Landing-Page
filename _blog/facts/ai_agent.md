# AI Voice Agent (from https://callforges.com/ai-agent.html)

## What the AI voice agent is
URL: https://callforges.com/ai-agent.html
The AI voice agent is an assistant that answers your phone line in a natural voice. When your team logs off, it picks up: it talks with the caller, answers from your own help docs in the caller's language (30+ languages), and books a callback for the morning or hands the call to your team when a person is needed. It works 24/7: nights, weekends and holidays.

## Setting up the AI agent
URL: https://callforges.com/ai-agent.html
Three steps: add your docs (help articles, policies, FAQs; the agent answers from these and nothing else); choose when it answers (after hours, on weekends, or whenever your routing says); turn it on (pick the number, and calls start getting answered).

## When the agent picks up and what it can answer
URL: https://callforges.com/ai-agent.html
You decide when it picks up with call routing; most teams send calls to the agent after hours, so nights and weekends are covered. It answers questions covered by the help docs, policies and FAQs you give it, in 30+ languages. You own those sources, so keep them current. If it doesn't know, it books a callback instead of guessing.

## The agent never pretends to be a person
URL: https://callforges.com/ai-agent.html
Every call starts with the agent saying it's an automated assistant. Callers can reach a person or leave a message at any point. It only answers from the sources you give it.

## AI agent minutes and billing
URL: https://callforges.com/ai-agent.html
You pay for the minutes the agent talks, at $0.12 per minute. Agent minutes are separate from calling minutes and pooled across your account: Starter pays $0.12 per agent minute, Business includes 100 minutes per user a month, and Scale includes 250 minutes per user a month. Extra agent minutes are $0.12 each. After a transfer to your team, the rest of the call uses normal calling minutes. You can try the agent free for 14 days with no card to start.
