# CallForges overview (from https://callforges.com/)

## What CallForges is
URL: https://callforges.com/
CallForges is a dialer, auto dialer and AI voice agent for teams calling the US, UK and Australia. It gives a team local US, UK and Australian numbers, an auto dialer, and an AI agent that picks up 24/7.
The product has five parts:
- Numbers: US, UK, Canada and Australia numbers in a minute, plus numbers in 50+ more countries. You can port your own numbers, even during your trial.
- Auto Dialer: one contact after another, with voicemails dropped for you. The next contact rings when you hang up.
- AI Notes: calls written down, with an AI summary synced to your CRM.
- AI Voice Agent: picks up at night, answers from your docs and books the callback for the morning. It answers 24/7 in 30+ languages, from your own docs.
- Routing: the right team on the first ring. Route by country, time zone or VIP status; for example sales to the UK sales team, support to a UK and EU queue, and after hours to the AI agent.
CallForges follows every contact's local time, wherever they are, so one team can call across time zones.
The free trial is 14 days, with 50 minutes inbound and 50 minutes outbound, and no card is required.

## Devices and hardware
URL: https://callforges.com/
You do not need new hardware. CallForges runs on desktop, web, iOS and Android.

## Keeping your numbers (porting)
URL: https://callforges.com/
Yes, you can keep your numbers. CallForges handles the port for you, and your old system keeps working until the move completes. Porting a US number costs $10, and you can start a port during your free trial.

## Whether callers know the AI agent is AI
URL: https://callforges.com/
Yes. The agent introduces itself as a virtual assistant and hands off to a person on request.

## International calling cost
URL: https://callforges.com/
Each user gets 100 to 1,000 free minutes each month, depending on the plan, for calls to and from the US, UK, Australia and Canada, inbound and outbound. After that, and for other countries, calls are billed per minute and each call's cost shows in Billing after the call. See the fair usage policy.

## Price compared with Aircall
URL: https://callforges.com/
The home page compares the Starter plan with Aircall Essentials: CallForges Starter starts at $15 per seat a month, billed annually, with no seat minimum, a free local number, 100 free minutes a month and AI transcripts billed per minute. Aircall Essentials is $30 per seat with a 3-seat minimum (Aircall list prices, September 2026, as shown on the page).
