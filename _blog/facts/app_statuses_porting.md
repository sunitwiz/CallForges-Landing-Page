# In the app: port request statuses (written from the app's own screens, 31 October 2026)

## Port request statuses: from Draft to Waiting for signature
URL: https://callforges.com/numbers/port#statuses-start
Ask: What does Waiting for signature mean on a port request?
Each port request on the Porting tab of Phone Numbers has one status:
- Draft: you are still filling it in. Nothing has gone to your current provider yet. Click Continue to finish it.
- On request: it went to our porting team, who will contact you. Nothing else happens in the app.
- Submitting: just submitted; we are waiting for the carrier to confirm it received the request. The first step reads "Confirming with the carrier".
- In review: the carrier is checking the request. Next, we e-mail the letter of authorization to your authorized representative: the person on your side you named in the application, not anyone at your current provider.
- Waiting for signature: we e-mailed the letter of authorization to your authorized representative, the person you named in the application (it comes from our carrier partner). The port goes ahead once they sign it; an unsigned request expires after 30 days.

## Port request statuses: In progress, Action required and Completed
URL: https://callforges.com/numbers/port#statuses-progress
Ask: What does Action required mean on a port request?
- In progress: your current provider is releasing the numbers, and each number gets a port date.
- Action required: your current provider rejected some numbers, and the reason shows beside each one. The other numbers carry on. Fix the details and send those numbers again, for example with Duplicate in the row's menu.
- Completed: the numbers are in your workspace and work like numbers you bought.
The request page shows the steps Submitted, Waiting for signature, In progress, Port date set and Completed.

## Port request statuses: Canceling, Canceled, Expired and Failed
URL: https://callforges.com/numbers/port#statuses-closed
Ask: What do Canceled, Expired and Failed mean on a port request?
- Canceling: we are waiting for your current provider to confirm the cancellation. The numbers stay with them.
- Canceled: the request was canceled and the numbers stay with your current provider.
- Expired: your authorized representative did not sign the letter of authorization within 30 days.
- Failed: the carrier could not take the request. Use Duplicate to retry; the copy keeps your details, and you add the account number, the PINs and the invoice again.
You can cancel a request while it is In review, Waiting for signature, In progress or Action required, but not shortly before a confirmed port date.

## Port request statuses: each number inside a request
URL: https://callforges.com/numbers/port#number-statuses
Ask: What do the statuses next to each number in a port request mean?
Once a request has gone to the carrier, each number in it shows its own status:
- In review: being checked. Waiting for signature: waiting for your authorized representative to sign the letter of authorization.
- Submitted: your current provider is processing it. Port date set: the move date is scheduled.
- Rejected: your current provider refused it; the reason shows on the row.
- Finishing setup: moved, and it appears in your numbers within minutes. Ported: moved and ready, with a link to open the number.
- Canceling or Canceled: the cancellation is being confirmed, or the number is not moving.
The date column reads "No port date yet", or "No port date" once the request closed without porting. While you enter numbers in the wizard, each one shows "Not checked yet", "This number can be moved", or "This number can't be moved here automatically" with a link to submit it on request.
