# In the app: the Dashboard (written from the app's own screens, 31 October 2026)

## Dashboard: the tabs, the date filter and who sees whose calls
URL: https://callforges.com/dashboard#tabs
Ask: Who sees whose calls on the Dashboard?
The Dashboard has the tabs Overview, Analytics, Outbound, Inbound and Team. The date filter offers Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days (the default), Last 30 days, This month, Last month and Custom range (up to 1 year), in your own time zone. Most counts compare with the period of the same length just before it, and clicking one opens the matching calls in Call History.
- Owners and admins see everyone's calls, with the Users and Teams filters, the Team tab, Number health, the Queue card and the service level.
- Users see only their own calls: the calls they placed, answered or were rung for. They get My day on Overview, "Your campaigns", and Called back instead of the service level.
- Everyone can filter by Numbers.

## Dashboard: Calls placed, Answer rate, Talk time and Voicemails on Overview
URL: https://callforges.com/dashboard#overview
Ask: How is Answer rate calculated on the Dashboard?
- Calls placed: outgoing calls placed, every parallel line included. Calls refused before dialing don't count.
- Answer rate: calls answered by a person divided by calls placed.
- Talk time: total talk time on answered calls, hold included.
- Voicemails: calls that reached voicemail.
- What changed: up to 3 cards, such as a tile that rose or fell, your busiest weekday, and (for owners and admins) who made the most calls.
- Call volume: calls per day, answered inside the total. Outcomes: Answered by a person, No answer, Busy, Failed and By voicemail.
- Campaign performance: the top 5 campaigns by calls, with Calls, Answered and Rate. Top numbers: the 3 numbers that placed the most calls, with their answer rate.
- Workspace: your contacts, lists, numbers and team at a glance.

## Dashboard: My day
URL: https://callforges.com/dashboard#my-day
Ask: What does My day show on the Dashboard?
My day is on Overview for users, marked Today. It shows your Dials and Conversations against today's goal, and your Talk time and Positive outcomes when goals are set for them. "N missed calls to call back" counts missed calls (voicemails included) from the last 24 hours that rang you and that nobody has called back. "Your best hour" is the weekday and hour with your highest answer rate over the last 30 days, once you have at least 10 dials in it.

## Dashboard: the Analytics tab
URL: https://callforges.com/dashboard#analytics
Ask: What does the Analytics tab on the Dashboard show?
Analytics puts calls in and out together.
- Outbound calls (as Calls placed), Inbound calls, Missed calls (missed incoming calls, voicemails included) and Talk time (answered outgoing and incoming talk).
- Calls over time: the share of Outbound, Inbound and Missed, and each per day.
- Numbers: Outbound, Inbound and Missed per number. Team members (owners and admins): each user's Last active, Outbound, Inbound answered, Missed (rang them) and Talk time.
- Voicemail: Voicemails received, Called back (the share of voicemails called back within 24 hours) and Avg time to call back.
- Missed today: today's missed incoming calls, each marked Called back or Not called back yet. Busy hours: calls by weekday and hour, for All, Outbound, Inbound or Missed.

## Dashboard: Dials, Conversations and Positive outcomes on the Outbound tab
URL: https://callforges.com/dashboard#outbound
Ask: What are Dials, Conversations and Positive outcomes on the Outbound tab?
- Dials: outgoing calls placed. Answer rate: answered divided by dials. Conversations: answered calls with over 60 seconds of talk. Positive outcomes: answered calls given a disposition marked positive.
- From dial to outcome: Dials, then Answered, Conversations and Positive outcomes, for All, Manual, Power or Parallel, and where the other dials ended (for example Picked up, no user free, in a parallel campaign).
- Call outcomes: Answer rate (answered by a person) and Pick-up rate (a person or voicemail picked up), with every outcome under Picked up and Not picked up.
- Call dispositions: dials per disposition chosen after the call, and No outcome picked.
- Best time to call: answer rate by weekday and hour in the lead's local time; cells with under 10 dials stay grey.
- Call handling: Avg call duration, Avg hold time, Avg wrap-up time, Voicemails left and Attempts per answer. Call attempts: contacts grouped by how many times they were called, with their answer rate.

## Dashboard: Number health
URL: https://callforges.com/dashboard#number-health
Ask: What does "May be labelled spam" mean in Number health?
Number health is on the Outbound tab for owners and admins. It shows each number's pick-up rate over the last 7 days against the 4 weeks before, whatever the date filter says; parallel lines cut before an answer are left out.
- Healthy: the number's pick-up rate is normal.
- Too few calls: not enough recent or earlier calls to judge.
- May be labelled spam: its pick-up rate fell to 60% or less of both its own earlier rate and your other numbers' rate, by 5 points or more. Consider resting this number.

## Dashboard: Service level, Answered and Missed on the Inbound tab
URL: https://callforges.com/dashboard#inbound
Ask: How is Service level calculated on the Inbound tab?
- Service level (owners and admins): incoming calls answered within your set time divided by calls offered to the team, against your target, by default 80% answered within 30 seconds.
- Answered. Missed: voicemails included. Avg answer time: from the first ring to the answer. Called back (for users): missed calls called back within 24 hours.
- Missed calls by reason: Voicemail, Hung up in queue, Hung up before ringing, Not picked up, Outside working hours, Nobody available or Other, each split into Called back, Not called back and Missed under 24 hours ago.
- Queue (owners and admins): Callers who waited, Average wait, Longest wait and the share who hung up while waiting.
- Busy hours, Incoming calls per day (Answered, Voicemail, Missed, no voicemail) and Call handling (Avg call duration, Avg hold time, Transferred and Transfer answered).

## Dashboard: the Team tab and the leaderboard
URL: https://callforges.com/dashboard#team
Ask: What do the columns of the Team leaderboard mean?
The Team tab is for owners and admins.
- Users dialing: users with at least one dial. Dials per user: dials divided by users dialing. Conversations. On goal today: users with a goal who met both their dials and conversations goals today.
- The leaderboard (Export CSV) shows each user's Today's goal, Total calls, Dials, Answer rate, Conversations, Talk time, Avg handle (talk and wrap-up per handled call), Available time (set to Available, inside working hours, with CallForges open), Positive, Incoming answered, Incoming missed, Dialing time (campaign sessions, start to end) and Days on goal.
