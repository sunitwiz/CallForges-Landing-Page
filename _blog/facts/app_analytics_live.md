# In the app: Live, the wallboard, goals and the weekly digest (written from the app's own screens, 31 October 2026)

## Live: who is on a call, who is waiting and today so far
URL: https://callforges.com/live#floor
Ask: What does the Live page show?
Live shows who is on a call, who is waiting, and how today is going. It is for owners and admins, and anyone whose role is given live monitoring.
- The strip counts live calls, waiting callers, and users available, in wrap-up, away and offline, with the Service level today against its target.
- Each user's card shows On a call, Available ("Ready for the next call"), Wrap-up, Away with the reason, or Offline ("Not signed in"), and how long they have been in it; a live call reads Ringing, Talking, Transferring or On hold with a timer. The footer shows dials and conversations against today's goal.
- Waiting lists each caller in a queue with their wait, their place in line and the number they called; Over SLA means they waited longer than your service level time.
- Today: Dials, Dials answered, Answered (incoming) and Missed (incoming). The floor refreshes every 5 seconds and Today every 30 seconds.

## Live: the TV wallboard
URL: https://callforges.com/live#wallboard
Ask: What does the TV wallboard show?
Open wallboard on Live opens "Today on the floor" in its own tab, for a TV. It never rings.
- Dials (with how many were answered), Conversations ("Talked over a minute"), Service level against its target, and Waiting (the longest wait, or Nobody waiting).
- Race to today's goal: each user's dials against their goal, 8 users a page, changing every 15 seconds.
It shows users' names and counts only, never a contact's name or number, and warns when its figures are more than 30 seconds old.

## Daily goals and the service level
URL: https://callforges.com/live#goals
Ask: How do I set daily goals and the service level?
Owners and admins set them in Settings → Analytics, used by the Dashboard and Live.
- Service level: the share of incoming calls answered within a time you choose. Answer within is 5 to 120 seconds, 30 by default; Target is 80% by default.
- Daily goals per user: Dials, Conversations, Talk time (minutes) and Positive outcomes; 0 means no goal.
- Goals per user: a personal goal for one user; Default means they follow the workspace goal.
Users see their own goals in My day.

## The weekly digest e-mail
URL: https://callforges.com/dashboard#weekly-digest
Ask: What is the weekly digest e-mail?
Every Monday from 08:00 in the owner's time zone, the owner gets last week's summary, with every admin copied, if the workspace made a call that week. It shows Dials (with the daily average), Answer rate, Conversations (over 60 seconds of talk) and Positive outcomes against the week before, the top users by conversations with their goal days, and numbers at risk of being labelled as spam. It is on by default; owners and admins turn it off in Settings → Notifications with Weekly digest e-mail, or with the link in the e-mail.
