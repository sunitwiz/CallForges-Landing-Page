Why teams look for a Dialpad alternative

Dialpad's AI is strong, and it comes with the cheapest plan. Connect Standard, at $15 per user a month billed annually or $27 monthly, includes real-time transcripts and instant call summaries, which Dialpad calls AI Recaps. If your team wants calls, meetings and messaging in one app with AI notes on every call, Dialpad suits you.

No Connect plan lists a dialer. Dialpad's sales product, Sell, lists local presence dialing and voicemail drop from its Advanced plan, at $59 per user a month billed annually, and the cheapest plan that lists a Power Dialer is Support Essentials, in its contact center product, at $89. Premium plans on Sell and Support are annual only (Dialpad pricing, checked November 2026).

The other reasons come from reviews, so read them as what some customers say. On Trustpilot, support is the most common theme in Dialpad's 1-star reviews, with reviewers passed between chat, phone and email (Trustpilot, Dialpad reviews, read November 2026). Others write about contracts: a license still billed after the reviewer asked to remove it before renewal, and a refund still owed weeks after canceling (Trustpilot, Dialpad 1-star reviews).

What to check before you switch

Ask every vendor these five questions in writing before a trial ends.

  • Is there a seat minimum? A three-seat minimum makes a two-person team pay for three. Dialpad's pricing page doesn't list one, so ask.
  • Which plan has the dialer? Ask for a power dialer, which rings the next contact when you hang up, and a parallel dialer if your reps work big lists.
  • How is the AI billed? It can sit in the seat price, come as a per-user add-on or be billed per minute. Per minute suits a team that transcribes some calls; if you transcribe every call, compare the monthly totals.
  • Which countries do your minutes cover? The footnote under "unlimited" names them. If you call Australia or the UK, get that rate.
  • When does your contract renew? Note the renewal date and notice period, and keep the old service until your numbers have ported, or the port can stall or fail.
