Why sales teams look for a JustCall alternative

JustCall's dialer sits one plan above its starting price. Team, at $29 per user a month billed annually ($39 monthly), has no power dialer, and Pro has one at $49 ($69 monthly). Both need at least two seats. AI Review Assist, the add-on with call summaries, costs $9 a user on either plan and comes included on Pro Plus at $89. Ringing several leads at once takes SalesPro, whose predictive dialer runs up to 10 lines on a custom quote (JustCall pricing).

Read "unlimited" with the fine print open. The plan cards promise unlimited minutes under a fair usage policy, and the credits tab on the same page lists 1,000 outbound minutes per user, pooled. The included minutes leave out international calls and call forwards, along with toll-free and premium-rate numbers (JustCall pricing).

Reviewers add two more reasons. On JustCall's one-star Trustpilot page, they describe support that's hard to reach, with a bot before a person, and billing trouble, such as a number billed for months after they lost it and no refund (Trustpilot, JustCall one-star reviews). Each is one customer's account, and that filter shows only the angriest, so read the full Trustpilot page too.

What to check before you switch

Price each option for your team today, and check:

  • Seat minimum: a two-rep team on a three-seat plan pays for a rep it doesn't have.
  • Dialer: which plan has it, and whether it rings one lead or several.
  • AI add-ons: whether call summaries come with the plan or cost extra.
  • Minutes: which countries are included, and what "unlimited" excludes.
  • Porting: the fee, and whether your old line works until the move completes.
  • Contract: monthly or annual, and whether you can drop seats before renewal.

Switching from JustCall to CallForges

Start with your numbers: open Phone Numbers and click Port number. A US number costs $10 to port, and you can start during the free trial. Keep your JustCall account open until the port completes; your number keeps working there in the meantime (Port your number to CallForges).

The trial runs 14 days with no card, a free local number and 50 inbound and 50 outbound minutes, and calls stop at 5 minutes until you add a card. Campaigns start with your plan, and one Starter seat is $19 billed monthly.

Then learn the campaign form:

  1. Open Campaigns and click Create campaign.
  2. Under Dialer Type, choose Parallel Dialer and set Number of parallel calls, or choose Power Dialer to ring one lead at a time.
  3. Under Call Via, pick the number your leads see.
  4. Under Audience, click Upload leads and pick your CSV file. Click Create Campaign, then Start Campaign.
