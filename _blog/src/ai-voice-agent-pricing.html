How AI voice agents are priced

Vendors charge in four ways, and one quote can combine two, such as a bundle with a setup fee. The cheapest one depends on how many calls the agent takes and how long they run.

  • Per minute: you pay for the time the agent spends on calls, so a quiet month costs little.
  • Per call: a flat price for each call the agent answers. Long calls come out cheap, and a caller who hangs up after ten seconds costs as much as a five-minute one.
  • A monthly bundle: a fee covers a set number of minutes or calls, with an overage rate after that. The overage rate sets what your busiest month costs, so read it first.
  • A setup fee: some services charge once to build the agent and load your information. Ask what a later change to your hours or prices costs.

The per-minute prices you'll find aren't always comparable. A market-research publisher that compared self-serve list prices in October 2026 found AI voice agents at about $0.07 to more than $0.30 a minute. It noted that some rates bundle the complete conversation while others charge separately for parts such as the language model, the voice and the phone line (New Market Pitch, 2026).

What's inside an AI agent minute?

Each minute of an agent call runs three pieces of software over a phone line. Speech recognition turns the caller's words into text, a language model writes the reply, and a synthetic voice reads it out. What is an AI voice agent? walks through one turn of a call.

Some vendors sell one rate for all of it. Others list their own fee per minute and bill the rest at other rates, so the headline price is the smallest number on your bill. Before you compare two quotes, get the same three answers from both:

  1. Does the rate cover the whole call, phone line included?
  2. What does the meter do once the agent hands the caller to a person?
  3. Is there a monthly platform fee or a minimum spend on top of the minutes?

Then put your own calls through each quote. Count last month's calls the agent would have taken, and guess the minutes it would talk on each. We'd start with two minutes a call for questions about hours or directions, or a message.
