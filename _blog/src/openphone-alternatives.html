What to check before you switch

Count a normal week first: dials per rep, and how many calls and texts go outside the US and Canada. Then ask each option:

  • Which plan has a dialer, and does it ring one lead at a time or several at once?
  • Are texts included, capped per user, or billed per message?
  • What does a call to London or a text to Sydney cost?
  • Is there a seat minimum?
  • How soon can moved numbers text? US carriers block business texts from unregistered local numbers (A2P 10DLC) on any system, so ask how long the review takes.
  • Which plan gets you a person on chat or phone?

We'd weigh the dialer most once two or more reps work call lists every day.

Switching from Quo to CallForges

Plan the move around texting: US numbers need a carrier review before they can send.

  1. Start the free trial: 14 days, no card, a free local number, and 50 inbound and 50 outbound minutes. US and Canadian numbers can't text in the trial. UK and Australian numbers can, paid from credits, once you add a card.
  2. Choose a plan and open the Compliance center. Once your Business profile is approved, click Add campaign on the Text messaging (A2P 10DLC) card. The carriers' review usually takes 1 to 3 weeks (Register for SMS in the US).
  3. Open Phone Numbers, click Port number and upload a recent Quo invoice. Moving a US number costs $10, and you can start in the trial. Your Quo line keeps working until the move completes, so keep Quo until the request shows Completed (Port your number to CallForges). We'd set the Preferred port date after the campaign is approved.
  4. A ported number follows the same texting rules as one you bought, so add it with Add to campaign on its SMS chip.

Your team then works in the Inbox, under Messaging. Pick a number and its conversations sort into Open, Assigned to me and Unassigned. Assign hands one to a teammate, and Close files it until the contact texts again. A contact who replied STOP shows Opted out (The Messaging Inbox).

For the dialer, open Campaigns, click Create campaign, and under Dialer Type pick Power Dialer or Parallel Dialer.
