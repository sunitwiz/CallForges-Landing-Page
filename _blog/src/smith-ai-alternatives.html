Switching to CallForges

The free trial runs 14 days with no card, and includes a local US, UK, Canada or Australia number and 50 minutes each way (calls stop at 5 minutes until you add a card).

  1. Write down what callers ask, like hours and fees: the agent answers only from your docs.
  2. Open Workflows, click New workflow, pick Business hours + voicemail and click Use template. Set the Time rule to your hours, and send At any other time to the AI agent or to a phone menu with an emergency key (Build a call workflow).
  3. Click Publish, then Numbers, pick your number and click Save numbers.
  4. Open Phone Numbers and click Port number. A US number costs $10, charged once its port date is confirmed, and you can start during the trial. Your old service keeps working until the move completes (Port your number).

Is there a free Smith.ai alternative?

Smith.ai has the only free-forever plan here: its AI answers 25 calls a month for $0, then $3.00 a call. Goodcall offers a demo agent, Ruby money back, and the rest a trial of 7 to 14 days.

What is cheaper than Smith.ai?

In our 300-call example, every AI-only service here costs less than Smith.ai's $500 AI plan. At 25 calls a month or fewer, Smith.ai's free plan beats them all.

Both put people on your phone, Smith.ai per call and Ruby per minute. At 3 minutes a call, our example costs $3,127.50 on Ruby and $2,100 on Smith.ai's human Pro plan.
