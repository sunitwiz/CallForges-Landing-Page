Why teams look for an Aircall alternative

For a small team, the first reason is the seat count. Aircall says most of its plans need at least three licenses (Aircall help center, checked November 2026), so two reps pay for three. Essentials, at $30 per license a month billed annually, has no power dialer; that starts on Professional at $50 (Aircall pricing, checked November 2026). The page says annual billing saves 25% but doesn't show a monthly price.

Then come the add-ons. The same page lists AI Assist Pro, for real-time coaching, at $49 per seat a month and Analytics+ at $15, and extra numbers cost $6 a month each. On Essentials, call summaries need an AI Assist add-on, and the page doesn't show its price. If you pay annually upfront, Aircall locks your licenses for the year and doesn't refund a user you remove mid-cycle (Aircall help center).

Reviewers on Trustpilot describe bills above the price they signed up for, and quotes with AI licenses they hadn't asked for. Others write about annual plans that renewed before they could act, charges after they cancelled or moved their numbers out, and support tickets closed before the problem was fixed (Aircall reviews on Trustpilot, one-star reviews). Treat them as individual accounts.

Aircall's routing extras, such as whisper and barge, sit on Professional at $50. Three reps who only dial pay for features they won't use.

What to check before you switch

Start with your renewal date. If you paid Aircall annually upfront, plan the switch for that date and start moving your numbers before it. Then ask each option:

  • What does a team of two or three pay once the seat minimum applies?
  • Which plan has the dialer, and does it ring one lead at a time or several at once?
  • Are call summaries on the plan, or an add-on per seat?
  • Are calls to the UK or Australia in your minutes, billed per minute, or a quote?
  • What does moving each number cost, and does your old line keep working until the move is done?
  • Can you remove a seat mid-year, and can you pay monthly?

We'd weigh the dialer question most. A rep making 100 dials a day gets more from the right dialer than from any add-on.

Switching from Aircall to CallForges

Moving your numbers is most of the work, and you can start on day one of the free trial. Open Phone Numbers and click Port number. US local numbers go through a wizard where you upload a recent Aircall invoice and list the numbers; other numbers go through Request a port. A US number costs $10, charged once your port date is confirmed. Your Aircall line keeps working until the move completes, so keep Aircall until the request shows Completed (Port your number to CallForges).

The trial lasts 14 days and needs no card. You get a free local number and 50 inbound and 50 outbound minutes, and calls stop at 5 minutes until you add a card. Campaigns start with your plan, so set up your numbers during the trial and choose a plan before your Aircall renewal (Start your free trial).

The screen your reps will care about is the campaign form. Open Campaigns, click Create campaign, and under Dialer Type choose Power Dialer for one lead at a time or Parallel Dialer to ring several and connect you to the first who answers. Then set Number of parallel calls. We'd start at two lines: once someone answers, the dialer hangs up on the rest, so each extra line can cut one more lead (How to set up a parallel dialer).
