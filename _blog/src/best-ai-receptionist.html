What makes a good AI receptionist for a small business

We judged each option on six things.

  • The billing model: per call, per minute, per unique caller, or a plan with a minute allowance. Per call suits long intake calls; per minute suits quick questions about hours. Our Smith.ai alternatives post runs the numbers for a 300-call month.
  • The question it can't answer. A good one admits it and offers a callback or a person; a bad one guesses and sounds certain.
  • The way to a person. Ask who it can transfer to, and whether that costs extra. A warm transfer, where your colleague hears who's calling first, beats a blind one.
  • Languages. Two of the tools below list English and Spanish only.
  • Hours. Check that you can choose when it answers. Most small teams start with nights and weekends, and holidays should follow the night path.
  • Disclosure. We'd only buy one that says it's an automated assistant up front and puts callers through to a person when they ask (What is an AI receptionist? explains why).

Setting up the CallForges AI agent for after hours

Most teams send the agent their night and weekend calls through a call workflow. After-hours answering for small businesses covers emergencies, and Build a call workflow has every step.

  1. Write down what callers ask when you're closed: hours, areas, fees. The agent answers only from the help articles, web pages, files or text you give it.
  2. Open Workflows, click New workflow, pick Business hours + voicemail and click Use template. Set the Time rule to your hours and time zone.
  3. On the At any other time path, add the AI voice agent step from the CallForges AI section of the step picker. If you want an emergency line too, an IVR menu whose emergency key goes to a Forward call on the on-call mobile can sit ahead of it.
  4. Add a Date rule so holidays take your night path.
  5. Click Save draft, then Publish. Click Numbers, pick your numbers and click Save numbers.
  6. After closing, call your number with one question your sources answer and one they don't, then ask for a person.
