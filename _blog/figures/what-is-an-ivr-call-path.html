How a call moves through an IVR

1 · The call comes in The IVR answers

Before anyone's phone rings

↓
2 · The menu plays Recorded, or read out by a voice "For service, press 1. For billing, press 2."
↓
3 · The caller answers Presses a key

Each key makes its own tone, so the system knows which one

↓
4 · The call goes on Down that key's path
Key 1Rings the service team
Key 2Rings billing
No key, or a wrong oneMenu again, then the front desk
