Why a law firm's calls never reach the matter

Most of a small firm's phone work happens outside Clio. A lawyer returns a client's call from a mobile in the car park, and the long call with opposing counsel never becomes a time entry. The intake coordinator calls weekend inquiries back from a spreadsheet, so nobody can tell on Tuesday whether the woman who wrote in about her car accident got a call.

Speed matters here too. Someone who filled in your form on Sunday night may have filled in two other firms' forms as well, and the first firm to call has the first conversation. A dialer fixes the speed. Logging every call to the matter fixes the record, and the billing that depends on it.

We'd fix the logging before buying any dialer. A dialer that gets through the list faster and logs none of it only makes the missing record worse.

Can a law firm use a sales dialer? The rules

Yes, for the right calls. Lawyers in each state follow that state's rules of professional conduct, and Rule 7.3 limits phoning someone to ask for paid work. Recording laws apply to everyone on the call, the firm included.

RuleWho it applies toWhat it says (November 2026)
North Carolina Rule 7.3Lawyers under North Carolina's rulesNo live person-to-person solicitation, a live phone call included, when a significant motive is the lawyer's pecuniary gain, unless the person is a lawyer, has a family, close personal, or prior business or professional relationship with the lawyer or firm, or routinely uses that type of legal service for business. A reply to a request for information isn't a solicitation (Comment 1), and text messages aren't live contact (Comment 2). Nobody who has said they don't want to be solicited may be.
Pennsylvania Rule 7.3Lawyers under Pennsylvania's rulesThe same ban with two exceptions only: lawyers, and people with a family, close personal or prior professional relationship. Text messages count as live contact (Comment 2). A defendant in a domestic relations case can't be solicited until proof of service is on the docket.
18 U.S.C. 2511(2)(d)Anyone recording a call under federal law, other than officials acting under color of lawRecording is lawful when you're a party to the call or one party has given prior consent, unless the purpose is a criminal or tortious act.
Fla. Stat. 934.03(2)(d)Calls covered by Florida lawRecording is lawful when all of the parties have given prior consent.

Each state's bar writes its own Rule 7.3, and each state its own recording law, so check the states you practice in and the states your callers are in. Pennsylvania's rule has no business exception like North Carolina's, so a corporate firm there can't count on phoning businesses that hire lawyers.

This is general information, not legal advice.

Who a law firm's dialer should call

The calls worth making are the ones people asked for, and North Carolina's comment says a reply to a request isn't a solicitation. Say your firm gets 40 web inquiries over a weekend: on Monday they're a list, and a dialer gets through it before lunch. Other good lists are the missed calls from the day before, consultations that didn't show, and past clients with a new matter coming up. Other lawyers, and referral partners you already work with, sit on the exception lists in both states.

We'd use the power dialer for all of these. It calls one person at a time, so everyone who asked for a call hears a lawyer who has their file open, and every call gets its own phone call in Clio. Leave a short voicemail for anyone who doesn't pick up, recorded once, so they know who called and how to reach you.

Keep a do-not-call list for the firm. Under both rules above, someone who has said no shouldn't hear from you again.

How to set up CallForges with Clio

Clio logging comes with the Business and Scale plans, for Clio Manage accounts in the United States, and your Clio plan needs to allow third-party apps. An owner or admin sets it up (Clio integration):

  1. Open Integrations, choose Clio and click Connect. Under Where is your Clio account?, choose United States, click Continue to Clio and allow access. Connect as a Clio administrator, so CallForges can find every contact.
  2. On Map users, click Auto-map by e-mail, match anyone left and click Save mappings. Calls are logged for users matched to a Clio user.
  3. On Settings, choose Call activity or Don't log for each kind of call, from outbound answered to voicemails. Under What each phone call includes, pick Link to the recording, Link to the transcript and AI summary, and turn on Add a time entry for each call. Turn on Make time entries billable if you bill phone time. Click Save changes.
  4. Open Campaigns, click Create campaign and choose Power Dialer under Dialer Type. Turn on Respect Do-Not-Call (DNC) lists and Use the lead/contact's local time zone to call during their active hours (Create a campaign).

On a call that reaches voicemail, click Voicemail, pick your recorded message and click Drop voicemail. Each number's Recording & transcripts settings decide whether its calls are recorded, and the first time someone presses Record, an owner or admin confirms that callers are told with Confirm and record.

When a client has two open matters, the call stays on the contact, and Move to a matter moves the phone call and its time entry to the one you pick. A new caller's call waits in Clio's Inbox → Logs until you click Add to Clio. Log past calls sends up to a year of earlier calls.

Common questions

Can CallForges bill phone time to a matter in Clio?

Yes. With Add a time entry for each call on, every call with talk time gets one time entry, linked to the phone call and to its matter, dated in the lawyer's Clio time zone. Entries are non-billable until you turn on billable ones, and a switch per user turns them off for one lawyer.

Does it work with Clio Grow?

It works with Clio Manage. A person who exists only in Clio Grow counts as an unknown number, so the call is logged in Clio without a contact until you add one. One CallForges workspace connects to one Clio account.

Where do recordings and transcripts live?

In CallForges, behind links that only signed-in users of your workspace can open. Turn on Save the recording in Clio to also store each recording in the matter's documents, or the contact's when the call has no matter.
