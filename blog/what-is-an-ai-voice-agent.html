What is an AI voice agent?

A lead fills in your website form at 9:40 on a Tuesday night, and by morning they've booked with whoever called first. An AI voice agent is software that holds a phone conversation in a natural voice. It listens, answers from the information you give it, and hands the call to a person or books a callback when it can't help. It can answer your calls, and it can make calls for you.

CallForges team · 22 November 2026

Key takeaways
  • An AI voice agent talks with callers in plain speech, works from information you give it, and passes the rest to a person.
  • It can answer inbound calls, like an AI receptionist, or place outbound calls, such as calling back a new lead.
  • The rules care about the AI part: tell people they're talking to AI, and in the US, outbound AI calls need the right consent.

How an AI voice agent works

An AI voice agent strings three pieces of software together and runs them fast enough to feel like a conversation. Each turn of the call goes like this:

  1. Speech recognition turns what the caller says into text.
  2. A language model, the same kind of AI behind chat assistants, reads that text, looks up the answer in the information you gave it and writes a short reply.
  3. A synthetic voice reads the reply out, and the agent listens again.

Around that loop sit the parts that make it useful on a business line. It can transfer the call to a person, take a message or book a callback. The good ones also know what they don't know: when the answer isn't in your information, they say so instead of making something up.

That last part matters more than the voice quality. A language model can say something false in a calm, sure tone. So we'd judge any agent by two things: does it answer only from your sources, and what does it do when it can't?

Inbound and outbound: the two jobs

An AI receptionist is an AI voice agent doing one job, answering your phone. The same technology can also dial out. The jobs look like this:

Inbound: it answersOutbound: it calls
Typical callsAfter-hours calls, overflow when your team is busy, common questionsCalling back a new web lead, reminders, following up on old leads
What it needs from youYour hours, prices, policies and FAQsA list of people who agreed to be called, and what to say to them
Where it goes wrongCallers who can't reach a person, wrong answers from old documentsCalling people without consent, sounding like a robocall

For most small teams, inbound is the easier start. The calls are already coming in, and the ones you miss after hours are lost anyway. Our post What is an AI receptionist? covers that job in detail, including where callers hang up on it.

AI voice agent vs an IVR or a chatbot

People mix these up because all three are software talking to customers.

IVR phone menuWebsite chatbotAI voice agent
WhereOn the phoneIn a chat box on your siteOn the phone
How the caller answersPressing keys or saying a short wordTypingTalking normally
What it can doRoute the call to the right placeAnswer questions in textAnswer questions, take details, transfer or book a callback
Best forSending calls to sales, support or billingVisitors already on your websiteCalls nobody on your team is free to take

You don't have to pick one. A common setup puts a short phone menu first, sends routine questions to the agent, and keeps a person on the "press 1 for an emergency" option.

Do the rules treat an AI voice agent differently?

Yes, in two ways. Both are laws, and both depend on where you and the people you call are.

Telling people it's AI

Article 50 of the EU AI Act says AI systems meant to talk with people must be designed so people learn they're dealing with AI, unless that's obvious from the context, at the latest at their first interaction (EU AI Act, Article 50). It applies from 2 August 2026 (EU AI Act, Article 113). Outside the EU, we'd still say it in the first sentence. It costs nothing, and a caller who wants a person can ask straight away.

Outbound AI calls in the US

In February 2024 the FCC confirmed that the TCPA's restrictions on "artificial or prerecorded voice" calls "encompass current AI technologies that generate human voices" (FCC Declaratory Ruling, 2024). So an AI agent calling residential lines or mobile phones needs the called person's prior express consent, unless an exemption applies. We'd point an outbound agent at people who asked to hear from you, such as a lead who requested a callback on your form, and check your consent wording with a lawyer.

This is general information, not legal advice.

What an AI voice agent costs

Vendors charge for AI voice agents in different ways, by the minute, by the call or as a bundle in a plan. When you compare them, ask what counts as a minute, what happens to the minutes after the agent transfers a call, and whether you pay for calls the agent only answers to take a message.
