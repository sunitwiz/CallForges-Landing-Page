Missed call text back: how it works and how to set it up

Missed call text back sends an automatic text to anyone whose call you don't pick up, from the number they rang, a moment after the call goes unanswered. In CallForges you set it up by adding a text step after the voicemail on your call workflow's unanswered path, writing the message and publishing. Say you're 20 minutes into a demo when your business line rings: you can't take it, but the caller has your text before they've dialed the next company on their list.

How does missed call text back work?

Every call to your number runs through a call workflow, the set of rules that decides who rings and what happens next. The ring step has two exits, answered and unanswered, and the text sits on the unanswered one. If the idea is new to you, What is missed call text back? covers what it is and when it helps.

In CallForges the text can go in three places:

  • Straight after the ring runs out, with no voicemail: the caller gets the text and the call ends.
  • After the voicemail. The text goes once the greeting starts, without waiting for the caller to finish their message.
  • After a caller has waited in your hold queue as long as you allow.

We'd pick the voicemail route for almost everyone. Some callers would rather talk than type, and voicemail keeps that door open while the text reaches everyone else. Only another text or a delay can follow a text step, so voicemail has to come first. The step never answers or holds up the call; the text goes out a moment after the call reaches it.

Set up missed call text back in CallForges

Before you start

  • A paid plan. On the free trial, Send SMS is greyed in the list of steps and reads Not included in the trial. Upgrade to use it. You can still build and save the draft, and texting from the Inbox works on the trial.
  • A role that can edit and publish workflows.
  • A number that can text. On Phone Numbers, those numbers carry the SMS chip.

A US local number needs registering first, because US carriers block business texts from a local number that isn't registered. Open the Compliance center, find Text messaging (A2P 10DLC) and click Add campaign; you'll need an approved Business profile there first. Your first campaign costs $19.50 on your card to submit ($4.50 to register your business, $15 for the review), then $10 a month once approved. You can register during the trial, and since the review usually takes 1 to 3 weeks, start before you build anything. In Sample messages, name your business and add "Reply STOP to opt out". Register for SMS in the US walks through each step, and 10DLC registration fees and how long approval takes covers rejections.

Canadian local numbers text with no registration. In the UK and Australia only mobile numbers text, and toll-free numbers are call only in the US, Canada, the UK and Australia. Each text is paid from your credits.

Add the text to your workflow

  1. Open Workflows, click New workflow, pick Direct line (it rings the number's owner, then takes a voicemail) and click Use template.
  2. Click the Ring users or teams step. Check Ring to, then set Ring for: how long it rings before the call takes the Unanswered path. We'd keep it around 20 to 25 seconds.
  3. Click + under the Voicemail step on that path. To text at once, choose Send SMS under SMS. To wait first, choose Schedule rule, set Send after to a number of Seconds, Minutes, Hours or Days, then click + under it and choose Send SMS. On a Queue, use its Wait over path.
  4. Set Send SMS from to Incoming call number, so callers hear back from the number they rang. Every number on the workflow then has to be able to text; if one can't, pick a Fixed number that can.
  5. Write the Message. Click Insert field, pick First name and give it a fallback such as "there", so a caller who isn't in your contacts reads "Hi there". Leave out User first name: it's the user who answered, and on a missed call nobody did. Keep it to 160 characters, one text; these three examples are a good start.
  6. Click Publish; a draft never sends. If something needs fixing, Can't publish yet lists each problem with its step, such as a number that can't text or a field typed by hand instead of picked from Insert field.
  7. Click Numbers, pick your numbers and click Save numbers. A number on another workflow moves here when you click Take off and save.

Every step and setting is in Build a call workflow.

Send it at once, or wait a few minutes?

Send it at once unless your team calls back fast. The caller is choosing who to ring next, and your text should land while they're still holding the phone. If your people usually return missed calls within a couple of minutes, a two-minute delay keeps your text and your call from landing at the same moment.

A delay can run as long as 30 days, which suits a follow-up more than a missed call. For a missed call, keep it to minutes.

Texts already waiting keep the version they started with. Edit or republish the workflow and a waiting text still goes as written when the call came in. Delete the workflow and every waiting text stops and shows as not sent.

Test it before your customers do

Ring your number from your own cell phone while nobody's available to take it, or ask a colleague to let it ring out. Wait for the voicemail greeting and hang up. The text should arrive a moment later from the number you called. Reply to it, and the reply lands in the shared inbox, in that caller's conversation, under your workflow's text.

Then open the call in Call History. Its journey has a row for each text step, showing it as scheduled, sent or failed, and a failed text says why. These are the usual reasons:

  • The caller hid their number, so there was nothing to text.
  • They had replied STOP to one of your texts and haven't replied START since.
  • Your credits ran out.
  • The number the text was sent from can't text.

If the carriers' texting can't be reached for a while, CallForges keeps trying for up to 24 hours before it marks the text failed. Each call that reaches the step sends its own text, so three test calls bring three texts.

Before you go live, decide who reads the replies and how fast. A text back opens a conversation, and someone on your team has to finish it; a caller who texts back and hears nothing until tomorrow would have been better off with voicemail.

Missed call text back: common questions

How much does missed call text back cost?

In CallForges the text step comes with a paid plan, from $15 per user a month billed annually ($19 billed monthly). Each text is paid from your credits at the rate for the country it reaches, and a text waiting on a delay isn't charged until it goes. A US number adds the carriers' registration fees: $19.50 for your first campaign, then $10 a month for each approved campaign (see pricing).

Can I send the text only after hours?

Yes. Start the workflow with a time rule: during open hours the call rings your team, and outside them it goes to voicemail and then the text. A delay step won't do this, because it only sets how long the text waits.

Does it work with a UK, Canadian or Australian number?

Yes, with the right number. A Canadian local number texts with no registration. A UK or Australian local number can't text, so hold a mobile number in that country and send the text from it as a fixed number. Our guide to missed call text back in the UK covers which numbers work there.
