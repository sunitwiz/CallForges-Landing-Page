Aircall alternatives: cheaper options for small teams

The Aircall renewal is in your inbox. Your four reps are on Professional, the plan with the power dialer, and on annual upfront billing you can't drop a license until it renews. If your team mostly dials, we think CallForges is the better fit: no seat minimum, and both dialers on every plan. JustCall suits teams that text as much as they call, Dialpad teams that want meetings in one app, and Quo teams that need no dialer.

Why teams look for an Aircall alternative

For a small team, the first reason is the seat count. Aircall says most of its plans need at least three licenses (Aircall help center, checked November 2026), so two reps pay for three. Essentials, at $30 per license a month billed annually, has no power dialer; that starts on Professional at $50 (Aircall pricing, checked November 2026). The page says annual billing saves 25% but doesn't show a monthly price.

Then come the add-ons. The same page lists AI Assist Pro, for real-time coaching, at $49 per seat a month and Analytics+ at $15, and extra numbers cost $6 a month each. On Essentials, call summaries need an AI Assist add-on, and the page doesn't show its price. If you pay annually upfront, Aircall locks your licenses for the year and doesn't refund a user you remove mid-cycle (Aircall help center).

Reviewers on Trustpilot describe bills above the price they signed up for, and quotes with AI licenses they hadn't asked for. Others write about annual plans that renewed before they could act, charges after they cancelled or moved their numbers out, and support tickets closed before the problem was fixed (Aircall reviews on Trustpilot, one-star reviews). Treat them as individual accounts.

Aircall still suits some teams: three or more people running support and sales from a help desk or CRM, who use Professional's smart routing, whisper and barge. Three reps who only dial will use less of what they pay for.

What to check before you switch

Start with your renewal date. If you paid Aircall annually upfront, plan the switch for that date and start moving your numbers before it. Then ask each option:

  • What does a team of two or three pay once the seat minimum applies?
  • Which plan has the dialer, and does it ring one lead at a time or several at once?
  • Are call summaries on the plan, or an add-on per seat?
  • Are calls to the UK or Australia in your minutes, billed per minute, or a quote?
  • What does moving each number cost, and does your old line keep working until the move is done?
  • Can you remove a seat mid-year, and can you pay monthly?

We'd weigh the dialer question most. A rep making 100 dials a day gets more from the right dialer than from any add-on.

Four Aircall alternatives for a team of 3 to 5

CallForges

We built CallForges for teams that dial every day. There's no seat minimum, and every plan has the power dialer, the parallel dialer and voicemail drop. Starter is $15 per user a month billed annually ($19 billed monthly), with 100 free minutes per user for calls to and from the US, UK, Canada and Australia. Business, at $29, adds AI summaries, local caller ID and 500 free minutes. Transcripts are billed per minute on every plan, and the whole workspace shares one plan, so you can't mix Starter and Business seats (CallForges pricing).

JustCall

JustCall fits outbound teams that text as much as they call. Team is $29 per user a month billed annually ($39 monthly). The power dialer starts on Pro at $49 ($69 monthly), and a predictive dialer needs SalesPro, a custom quote. Mind the two-seat minimum. On Team and Pro, call summaries come with AI Review Assist, a $9 per user add-on (JustCall pricing, checked November 2026).

Dialpad

Dialpad fits companies that want calls, meetings and messaging in one app, with AI transcripts and call recaps on the base plan. Connect Standard is $15 per user a month billed annually ($27 monthly), but no Connect plan lists a dialer. Sell Advanced, at $59, lists local presence dialing and voicemail drop, and "Power Dialer" first appears by name on Support Essentials at $89 per user a month, billed annually (Dialpad pricing, checked November 2026).

Quo (formerly OpenPhone)

Quo fits small US and Canada teams that mostly text and talk from shared numbers. Starter is $15 per user a month billed annually ($19 monthly), with no seat minimum stated, and Business, at $23, adds AI summaries for every call. No Quo plan lists a power or auto dialer, and calls outside the US and Canada are an add-on, billed per minute (Quo pricing, checked November 2026).

Switching from Aircall to CallForges

Moving your numbers is most of the work, and you can start on day one of the free trial. Open Phone Numbers and click Port number. US local numbers go through a wizard where you upload a recent Aircall invoice and list the numbers; other numbers go through Request a port. A US number costs $10, charged once your port date is confirmed. Your Aircall line keeps working until the move completes, so keep Aircall until the request shows Completed (Port your number to CallForges).

The trial lasts 14 days and needs no card. You get a free local number and 50 inbound and 50 outbound minutes, and calls stop at 5 minutes until you add a card. Campaigns start with your plan, so set up your numbers during the trial and choose a plan before your Aircall renewal (Start your free trial).

The screen your reps will care about is the campaign form. Open Campaigns, click Create campaign, and under Dialer Type choose Power Dialer for one lead at a time or Parallel Dialer to ring several and connect you to the first who answers. Then set Number of parallel calls. We'd start at two lines: once someone answers, the dialer hangs up on the rest, so each extra line can cut one more lead (How to set up a parallel dialer).

Common questions

Is there a free Aircall alternative?

None of the options here lists a free plan, and real calls run up carrier minutes, so a free plan would have to cap them. Our 14-day trial needs no card, but it's a trial, not a free plan, and dialer campaigns need a paid plan. JustCall's trial is also 14 days with no card (JustCall pricing), and Quo's is 7 days (Quo pricing).

What is cheaper than Aircall?

For a power dialer, CallForges Starter ($15) and Business ($29) cost less per user than Aircall Professional ($50), all billed annually. Dialpad Connect and Quo start at $15, half the price of Aircall Essentials, but neither lists a dialer on those plans.

Are there Aircall alternatives in Australia?

We can speak for CallForges. Each user gets one free local US, UK, Canada or Australia number, and free minutes cover calls to and from Australia. Extra Australian numbers are $5 a month for a local number, $10 for a mobile and $25 for toll-free, in US dollars. On Business and Scale, local caller ID calls a lead in Melbourne from an Australian number. The competitor prices in this post are in US dollars, so check each vendor's Australian pricing.
