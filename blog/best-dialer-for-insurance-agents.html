What makes a good dialer for insurance agents

Agents dial two kinds of list. Aged leads, quote requests bought weeks or months after someone made them, ring out more than they answer, so ringing several at once saves the afternoon. Callbacks and renewals pick up more often and deserve a line each. We judged each tool on five things:

  • Both modes: a power dialer calls one lead at a time, and a parallel dialer rings several and connects the first who answers (What is a parallel dialer?).
  • The dialer on a plan you'd buy. A three-seat minimum costs a solo agent two empty seats.
  • Calling hours in each lead's time zone, and a do-not-call check before every dial.
  • Caller ID people answer. Fix a number that already reads "Scam Likely" first (how to clean a flagged number).
  • Cover for an aged lead's 8 p.m. callback, after you've logged off.

The calling rules an insurance agent dials by

Each of these is a law or a regulator's rule, and they apply whatever dialer you use.

RuleWhat kindWho it applies toWhat it means for your list
The Telephone Consumer Protection Act and 47 CFR 64.1200A federal law, and the FCC's rules under itTelemarketing calls, and autodialed or prerecorded calls, to people in the USConsent, calling hours, do-not-call lists and abandoned calls.
Telemarketing Sales RuleThe FTC's ruleTelemarketers and sellers; insurance only "to the extent that such business is not regulated by state law" (FTC guide)Sync with the registry at least every 31 days. A quote request lets the company call for three months, and a purchase for 18 months after the last payment, transaction or shipment (FTC Q&A, 2026).
National Do Not Call RegistryThe list the FTC runs under these rulesSellers and telemarketers, through telemarketing.donotcall.govFive area codes of data are free. From October 1, 2026, each one after that costs $85 a year, up to $23,425 (FTC Q&A, 2026).

A quote request from six months ago is outside that three-month window, so scrub an aged list like any cold one.

You may still read about a 2025 "one-to-one consent" rule tying each consent to one seller. That rule is not in force. The Eleventh Circuit vacated it in January 2025, and the FCC restored the earlier definition of prior express written consent, effective August 29, 2025 (Federal Register, 2025). State laws can add limits of their own, so check the states your leads live in.

How to choose: solo agent, agency or call floor

A solo agent making 40 calls a day to people who asked to hear back can run them on the power dialer on CallForges Starter, for $15 a month billed annually, and everyone who answers reaches you.

Aged final expense leads change the math. Say you dial 400 in an afternoon and one in twelve picks up: on one line, that's eleven misses for every conversation. Three lines ring the misses side by side, but each pickup hangs up the lines still ringing, so 33 conversations can cut up to 66 leads. We'd start at two lines and re-dial cut leads once. Starter has the parallel dialer too, so the same seat runs both lists.

A ten-agent Medicare team in AEP needs both modes: warm leads and callbacks on the power dialer, the aged list on two or three lines. We'd take Business, with 500 free minutes and 100 pooled AI agent minutes per user, so an evening callback gets answered. Two-way texts and a shared team inbox come on every plan too.

Past ten agents on one campaign, CallForges Scale adds custom retry rules for dialer campaigns, advanced analytics and wallboards, at $45 per user a month billed annually.

Setting up an insurance campaign in CallForges

Campaigns start with a paid plan. Use the 14-day free trial, which needs no card, to submit your Business profile in the Compliance center and make your first calls on its free local number. Then, on a plan (Create a campaign):

  1. Open Campaigns and click Create campaign.
  2. Under Dialer Type, choose Parallel Dialer for aged leads, set Number of parallel calls to 2 and add a Voicemail drop. Re-dial cancelled leads starts at Never; set it to 1 to call cut leads again. For callbacks, choose Power Dialer. You can't change the type later.
  3. Under Call Via, pick the number your leads see, and set Cooldown duration between two calls.
  4. Under Audience, click Upload leads for your scrubbed CSV, or Select a list.
  5. Turn on Respect Do-Not-Call (DNC) lists and Use the lead/contact's local time zone to call during their active hours, then set Active Days, Start Time and End Time.
  6. Click Create Campaign. It opens as a Draft; click Start Campaign when you're ready.

The voicemail drop plays only when Auto machine detection is on in Settings → Dialer settings. Start during your leads' hours: a lead whose turn comes outside them shows Out of timezone and isn't called again in that campaign.

We'd pick CallForges. What should an agency do when a lead asks not to be called again?

Add the number to your agency's do-not-call list before the next campaign. The FTC's rule counts that list among the conditions of its safe harbor for calls made in error (FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule). A CallForges campaign with the do-not-call option on skips everyone on it.
