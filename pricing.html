Pricing questions.

Is there a seat minimum?

No. Start with one seat and add people when you need them.

Can I mix plans across my team?

Yes. Your SDRs can be on Business while your support team stays on Starter.

How are minutes counted?

Per minute, rounded up to the next full minute. Calls to and from the US, UK, Australia and Canada use your 1,000 free minutes first. See the fair usage policy for details.

Do unused free minutes roll over?

No. Your 1,000 minutes reset at the start of each billing month.

How is the AI voice agent billed?

Per minute the agent spends talking, at $0.12. Business and Scale include a pool of minutes each month. Time after a transfer to a person counts as a normal call.

Can I cancel?

Monthly plans cancel anytime. Annual plans can change or downgrade at renewal.
